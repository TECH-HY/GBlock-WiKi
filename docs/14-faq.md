# 14. FAQ

Short answers to the questions people ask most. Each answer points to the page
with the full picture. Functions marked _rolling out_ are part of the launch
design and are being switched on in stages.

---

## The basics

**What is GBlock?**
A private B2B market club. Founders show their companies, investors and owners
meet opportunities through mediated introductions, and every participant has
reputation at stake. See [What is GBlock](01-what-is-gblock.md).

**Is GBlock an exchange or a crypto product?**
No. It is not an exchange, broker, custodian, settlement agent or investment
advisor, and it has no token. A _block_ is a private package of assets or an
opportunity. See [What GBlock is not](01-what-is-gblock.md#what-gblock-is-not).

**Does GBlock promise returns or check what sellers say?**
No on both counts. GBlock promises no profit and does not verify the information
that members publish. Members do their own diligence. See
[Legal documents](13-legal-documents.md#important-notices).

**Is GBlock live?**
It is in pre-market testing before public launch. This wiki describes the product
as designed for launch. See [Launch scope and stage](09-roadmap-and-stage.md).

**Can anyone use it?**
Anyone can register. Market participation is earned. Availability by country opens
in stages, and payments and payouts are closed in sanctioned regions. See
[Who can use GBlock](01-what-is-gblock.md#who-can-use-gblock).

## Getting in

**How do I join?**
Register at <https://gblock.club> with your email and accept the Terms and the
Privacy Policy. You sign in with a one-time code from your email; there are no
passwords _(rolling out; a single-use link does the same until then)_. See
[Getting access](03-getting-access.md).

**Do I need an invitation?**
Not to register. You need vouches only to become a Resident. If you have no
contact inside GBlock, ask the community through the Telegram link on the site.

**How do I become a Resident?**
Join the waitlist with **Apply for Residency** and collect two vouches: the
first from a member who passed KYC, the second, decisive one from a Resident.
KYC for that Resident is not required _(rolling out; for now the Resident must
have passed KYC too)_.
Resident gives access to the dealflow, blocks, deal threads and the right to
vouch. See [Becoming a Resident](02-user-roles-and-statuses.md#becoming-a-resident).

**Is there another way to become a Resident?**
Only at launch: the operator appoints the first Residents, up to 50 members with
approved KYC, so that the network has members who can vouch. You cannot request
it.

**Do I need KYC?**
No, not to register and not to become a Resident. KYC gives the Verified trust
level, which adds trust in deals and unlocks selling without a guarantor, a longer
block window and larger payouts. Your documents stay with the external provider.
See [Trust and safety](07-trust-and-safety.md#kyc-and-identity).

## Status and reputation

**What is reputation, and can I buy it?**
It is your track record on GBlock. It cannot be bought; it is earned and it can
be lost. You start with 30 points, or 40 and a vouch gift if a member invited you.
See [Reputation economy](05-reputation-economy.md).

**What does my reputation tier change?**
It sets your reward rate (10% to 25%) and, for Residents, how many free
secondary-market Data Rooms you get. It does not replace identity checks: those
are the trust level.

**What is the difference between status, trust level and tier?**
Status is how you entered (Ambassador, Waitlist applicant, Resident). Trust level
is how proven your identity is (Anonymous, Vouched, Verified). Tier is your
reputation (Member to Vanguard). They are independent. See
[User roles and statuses](02-user-roles-and-statuses.md).

**Why would I vouch for someone?**
A vouch brings the candidate +10 reputation and you +5, but it puts your own
reputation at stake, and a withdrawn vouch costs you. Back only people you trust.

## Rewards and ambassadors

**What do ambassadors earn?**
A share of TECH HY's own revenue from people they brought in: scoring purchases,
Data Room subscriptions and voucher purchases. The direct rate is 10% to 25% by
tier, with sponsor overrides of 6%, 3% and 1% paid from the same 25% cap. See
[Rewards](05-reputation-economy.md#rewards).

**Do rewards come from deals?**
No. GBlock does not settle deals and takes no automatic commission on them.

**When can I take rewards out?**
A reward becomes releasable 30 days after the revenue it comes from. USDT
withdrawal opens later; until then released rewards stay in your reward balance
and can be moved into your payment balance with a 3% bonus. Once it opens, you
file a payout request, which the operator reviews. There is no instant
withdrawal. See [Payouts and rewards](10-payouts-and-rewards.md).

## Data Rooms, scoring and vouchers

**Who can open a Data Room?**
Any onboarded member can open one for their own company, and the first one is
free. A Resident can also create rooms about other companies, with no KYC. See
[Data Rooms](12-data-rooms-scoring-and-subscriptions.md#who-can-create-a-data-room-and-who-owns-it).

**Who can see a room?**
Only the people the owner chooses: named members, or everyone through
a public link. Viewing needs no KYC and no particular status. A public room shows
only a basic card; documents stay private unless the owner marks them public.

**Do I have to register to look at a public room?**
No. A visitor can look first and register later. See
[Silent Data Room referrals](11-silent-dr-referral.md).

**What is a scoring?**
A rubric-based evaluation of a project, built from the information in its Data
Room and the owner's answers, on Potential and Readiness, each from 0 to 150. It
costs $35 and is ordered by the company's founder of record from a Pro
room _(rolling out)_. The report is private by default; the owner chooses what to
publish. It is labelled Beta until calibration is complete, and it is information,
not advice or a guarantee.

**What is the difference between a Free and a Pro room?**
Documents, teammates, access grants and the public card work in both. Scoring, the
public project catalog, primary-market blocks and the AI data check _(rolling out)_
need Pro. Pro currently costs $35 per
month per room and includes one scoring voucher each month _(rolling out)_. See
[Free and Pro rooms](12-data-rooms-scoring-and-subscriptions.md#free-and-pro-rooms).

**What is a scoring voucher?**
A prepaid scoring, valid for three months and usable only in a Pro room. You get
one when you subscribe to a Pro room and another with each monthly renewal. See
[Scoring vouchers](12-data-rooms-scoring-and-subscriptions.md#scoring-vouchers).

**Is the information in a room checked by GBlock?**
No. The owner is responsible for it and answers with their reputation. Any member
can report false information, and the operator reviews the case; knowingly false
information can lead to sanctions up to a permanent ban. See
[Reporting a problem](07-trust-and-safety.md#reporting-a-problem).

**What happens if I stop paying for a Pro room?**
Nothing is deleted. If the retry after 48 hours fails, the room is frozen: people
who already have access can still read it, but you cannot grant new access or
upload until you pay or unfreeze it from **Subscriptions**. See
[When a subscription lapses](12-data-rooms-scoring-and-subscriptions.md#when-a-subscription-lapses).

## Deals and the market

**Who can sell a block?**
A Resident who is the founder of record of the company, with a valid scoring on
their own Pro Data Room, and who is either Verified (KYC) or Vouched (two
guarantees). The operator does not approve blocks in advance and steps in after a
complaint or dispute. See
[User roles and statuses](02-user-roles-and-statuses.md#trust-level).

**Does GBlock take a fee on deals?**
No automatic commission. A fee for an introduction applies only under separate
written terms. See [Launch scope and stage](09-roadmap-and-stage.md#not-at-launch).

**Do counterparties see who I am?**
Not before both sides agree. Names and contact details stay hidden, and the
conversation runs inside GBlock until the contract is signed. See
[Trust and safety](07-trust-and-safety.md#deal-threads-stay-inside-gblock).

**Does GBlock hold the money or assets in a deal?**
No. GBlock holds no assets and moves no deal funds.

## Money

**How do I top up?**
By card, from $10 to $100,000 per top-up. A top-up of $500 or more gives three
bonus scoring vouchers valid for 90 days _(rolling out)_. See
[Topping up the payment balance](10-payouts-and-rewards.md#topping-up-the-payment-balance).

**Can I get a top-up back?**
A refund of topped-up funds carries a 15% refund fee, and the price of any bonus
voucher you already used is deducted _(rolling out)_. No fee is charged where the
law requires a full refund or where the error is GBlock's. An approved refund is
processed within 10 business days. Bonus funds themselves cannot be withdrawn.
Details are on the same page.

**How do I withdraw rewards?**
USDT withdrawal opens later, and the wallet shows "Withdrawal opens later" until
then. Your rewards are not cancelled: they stay in the reward balance and can be
moved into the payment balance. Once it opens, you withdraw through a payout
request in USDT (BEP-20): minimum $50, one request per calendar
month, up to $150 per 30 days without KYC and up to $1,500 per month with KYC
_(rolling out)_. The operator responds within one business day, and approved
payouts are sent within 20 business days. See
[Requesting a payout](10-payouts-and-rewards.md#requesting-a-payout).

**A message says I must pay a fee or send my seed phrase to receive a payout.**
It is not from GBlock. Payouts never need a fee, a deposit or a seed phrase.

## Safety and your data

**How do I protect my account?**
Switch on two-factor authentication (optional) and keep an eye on the sign-in
alerts GBlock emails you. Sensitive actions ask for a fresh confirmation. See
[Account protection](07-trust-and-safety.md#account-protection).

**What happens if I stop logging in?**
After 14 days you get a warning; after 30 days the account sleeps and payments,
top-ups and new rooms freeze, while **subscriptions keep charging until you cancel
them**. After 180 days of sleeping it is deactivated. Nothing is deleted. See
[Inactivity](07-trust-and-safety.md#inactivity-deletion-and-your-data).

**How do I export or delete my data?**
From Settings or the **My data** page. The operator processes a deletion request
within 30 days. Deletion is postponed while you have active deals, unpaid
withdrawals, a debt, a positive balance or an active sanction.

**What happens if I am permanently banned?**
Paid services are not refunded. Rewards and bonuses are frozen for up to 10
business days and then cancelled, unless a complaint you file is upheld. Your
unspent payment balance is paid back on request, less the 15% refund fee and the
price of any bonus vouchers you used _(rolling out)_. You can appeal, and your
right to go to court stays. See
[Permanent ban](07-trust-and-safety.md#permanent-ban).

**How do I report a problem?**
Open **Report** in the cabinet and pick a category, or write to
`support@gblock.club`. The operator reviews the report, and your identity as the
reporter is not shown to the person you report. See
[Report a problem](04-user-journeys.md#report-a-problem).

**Where are the terms and disclaimers?**
On the [Legal documents](13-legal-documents.md) page, with the full texts.
