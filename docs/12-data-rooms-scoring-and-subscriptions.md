# 12. Data Rooms, scoring and subscriptions

This page explains how Data Rooms are priced, who owns them, what a scoring is
and how it is paid for, and what happens when a subscription lapses. For the
day-to-day flow of running a room, see
[User journeys - Manage a data room](04-user-journeys.md#manage-a-data-room).

Prices on this page are the current catalog prices. The operator can change a
price; existing subscribers get 30 days' notice before a new subscription price
takes effect.

---

## Who can open a Data Room, and who owns it

- **Any onboarded member in good standing** can open a Data Room for their
  own company: someone who has chosen at least one path in the cabinet and is
  not restricted.
- A room about **someone else's company** (the secondary market, including a
  company you back as an investor) can be opened only by a **full Resident**
  (KYC approved, two active vouches, approved waitlist request). Anyone else is
  turned away at creation.
- The room belongs to **you, its creator**. The company record behind it is a
  shared record, so several people can have their own rooms for the same
  company.
- A room can be **handed over** to another member only if the recipient accepts.
  Both the offer and the acceptance require re-authentication.
- If a creator is already opening a room for the same company at the same
  moment, you may be asked to try again before any payment is taken.

Uploading the first document to a room of your own company also switches on your
silent Data Room functions (payment account and scoring purchases). See
[User roles and statuses](02-user-roles-and-statuses.md#data-room-functions-the-founder-extension).

## Free and paid rooms

| Room | Cost |
|---|---|
| Your **first** Data Room | Free, in any market |
| **Free secondary-market rooms** | A **full Resident** (KYC approved, two active vouches, approved waitlist request) gets a number of free rooms for companies they do not own, on top of the first room, set by their reward-rate tier: Member 1, Core 2, Principal 3, Partner 5, Vanguard 8 |
| Every other room | A **Pro subscription** (currently $35 per month), charged automatically from your payment balance, with any shortfall taken from your saved card |

Good to know:

- The allowance follows your **locked weekly reward-rate tier**, not the live
  tier, so reputation gained in the middle of a week does not buy extra free
  rooms until the next snapshot. See
  [Reputation economy](05-reputation-economy.md#two-views-of-volume).
- There is no unlimited tier: above the allowance, a room is a paid
  subscription.
- A paid room is **created only after payment** is confirmed. The payment step
  shows the auto-charge consent.
- A subscription **includes one scoring voucher per month**. Vouchers are valid
  for 3 months.
- **Archiving** a room pauses its subscription: no charges while it is archived.

---

## Free and Pro rooms _(rolling out)_

A room is either **Free** or **Pro**. Documents, sections, team members, access
grants, watermarks, the access log, the public card and the referral funnel work
the same in both. Four things need Pro:

- ordering a scoring (and answering its questionnaire),
- appearing in the public project catalog,
- a primary-market block,
- the monthly scoring voucher.

A room is Pro while it is active and at least one of these applies:

- **a paid subscription** is running (currently $35 per month);
- **an operator promotion** is running: free Pro months granted by the operator,
  after which normal billing starts;
- **a voucher window** is open: applying a scoring voucher to a Free room gives
  30 days of Pro and one scoring.

Pro is never free because of your tier. Your first room and the free rooms from
your tier allowance stay Free rooms.

Good to know:

- A voucher window needs no card and does not renew. When it ends, the room goes
  back to Free and its scoring is hidden, not deleted, until the room is Pro
  again. You can set up a subscription during the window; the paid period then
  starts after the window ends and the rest of the window is not lost.
- A voucher applied to a room that is already Pro gives one scoring and does not
  extend Pro.
- A scoring order you already paid for, and a scoring credit you already hold,
  still complete if the room is no longer Pro.

## When a subscription lapses

Failure to pay never deletes your documents.

1. If a renewal fails, the charge is retried automatically after 24 and then 48
   hours while the room is still open.
2. After the last retry the room is **frozen**: investors with existing access
   can still read it, but you cannot grant new access or upload, and the room is
   hidden from the public catalog. A few further weekly retries are made after
   the freeze.
3. A cancelled subscription also leaves the room frozen, not deleted. You can
   unfreeze a room from the **Subscriptions** page.
4. If the owner is under a sanction, or the account is deactivated, charging
   stops and the room freezes until the reason ends. An open card dispute also
   stops charging.

**Cancelling.** You can cancel only in the cabinet, on the **Subscriptions**
page, in at most three screens, and every screen has a "cancel now" option. The
last cancellation screen shows a one-time retention offer of 50% off per room.
Members who have been inactive (14 days without signing in) get a warning email
three days before each charge. Account sleep does **not** pause billing, so
cancel a subscription you no longer want before you go quiet.

## Scoring

A scoring is a deterministic evaluation of a project on two axes: **Potential**
(0-150) and **Readiness** (0-150).

- **It needs a Pro Data Room.** A scoring cannot exist without the company's Data
  Room. The room collects the project information the scoring uses, and you
  start the scoring from there. Ordering a scoring requires the room to be Pro
  _(rolling out)_; see [Free and Pro rooms](#free-and-pro-rooms-rolling-out).
- **Price.** A scoring costs $35 (the current catalog price), paid from your
  payment balance or with a scoring voucher.
- **It does not expire with time.** A scoring stays valid while it was produced
  by the current scoring engine version. It is shown publicly only while the
  room is Pro _(rolling out)_: if the room goes back to Free, the scoring is
  hidden, not deleted, and shows again when the room is Pro again.
- **Public catalog.** A room appears in the public project catalog only with a
  valid scoring, a completed company profile (description, sector and similar
  public fields) **and** a Pro room. Without them the room still works for
  investors you granted access to.

### Scoring vouchers

- A voucher is a prepaid scoring. You can buy vouchers from your payment
  balance, and a Data Room subscription includes one per month.
- Vouchers are valid for 3 months.
- A voucher can be passed on **once**, only to the founder of record of a
  company, and only if that person accepts. A voucher that was transferred
  once cannot be transferred again.

## Effects of account status

| Situation | Effect on Data Rooms and scoring |
|---|---|
| **Sleeping account** (30 days without a login) | Payments, top-ups, scoring purchases and new Data Rooms are frozen. Existing rooms keep working; subscriptions keep charging. |
| **Revoked KYC** | New access grants, new Data Rooms and payouts are blocked until you re-verify. Existing rooms and grants keep working and are not archived. Re-approval lifts every block. |
| **Deactivated account** | Public listings are unpublished. Rooms are not deleted. |
| **Deletion at your request** | Data Rooms you own are removed with the account. |

See [Trust and safety](07-trust-and-safety.md) for the full inactivity and
deletion rules.
