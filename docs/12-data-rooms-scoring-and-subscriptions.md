# 12. Data Rooms, scoring and subscriptions

How Data Rooms are owned and priced, what a scoring is, and what happens when a
subscription lapses. For the step-by-step flow of running a room, see
[User journeys](04-user-journeys.md#open-a-data-room).

Prices are current catalog prices. The operator can change a price; existing
subscribers get 30 days' notice before a new subscription price applies.

---

## Who can create a Data Room, and who owns it

- **Any onboarded member in good standing** can create a Data Room for their own
  company.
- A room about **someone else's company** (for example a company you back as an
  investor) can be created by a **Resident**; KYC is not required. This is about
  creating a room, not about looking at one.
- The room belongs to **you, its creator**. The company record behind it is
  shared, so several people can have their own rooms for the same company.
- You can **hand a room over** to another member; it only happens if they accept.

Uploading the first document to a room of your own company also switches on your
founder-side functions: top-ups and scoring purchases. See
[User roles and statuses](02-user-roles-and-statuses.md#data-room-functions-the-founder-side).

---

## Who can see a room

Visibility is set only by the room owner. You choose who sees a room and its
contents: named members only, any member, or fully public, so that anyone can open
it without registering. Viewing needs no KYC and no particular status, and the
platform adds no viewing restriction of its own beyond the rules below for Free
rooms. This is also why every room link works as an invitation: a visitor can look
at a public room first and register later.

- **A public room shows a basic card.** Documents stay private unless you mark a
  document public yourself. Passports and KYC reports are never public.
- **A scoring report is private by default.** You can publish a reduced view
  (score, quadrant, dimensions, stage, short summary) or an extended view that
  adds strengths. The same view appears everywhere the report is shown.

---

## What you publish is your responsibility

Information in a Data Room, including the inputs to a scoring and any traction
figures, comes from the room owner. GBlock does not verify it. You answer for it
with your reputation: an investor or any other member can report false
information, the operator reviews the case, and knowingly false information can
lead to sanctions up to a lifetime ban, without a right to a refund.

## Free and paid rooms

| Room | Cost |
|---|---|
| Your **first** Data Room | Free |
| **Free secondary-market rooms** | A Resident can create free rooms about companies they do not own, by reputation tier: Member 1, Core 2, Principal 3, Partner 5, Vanguard 8 |
| Every other room | **Pro subscription**, $35 per month, charged from your payment balance with any shortfall taken from your saved card |

- Above the free allowance there is no unlimited tier; extra rooms are paid.
- A paid room is created only after payment is confirmed.
- A subscription includes **one scoring voucher per month**.
- **Archiving** a room pauses its subscription.

---

## Free and Pro rooms

_Rolling out._ A room is either **Free** or **Pro**. Documents, teammates, access
grants, the public card and the referral funnel work the same in both. Four
things need Pro:

- ordering a scoring,
- appearing in the public project catalog,
- a primary-market block,
- the monthly scoring voucher.

A room is Pro while one of these applies:

- a **paid subscription** is running ($35 per month);
- an **operator promotion** is running: free Pro months for one room, with a
  monthly voucher for that room, after which normal billing starts;
- a **Pro trial** is running: Pro for a set term on one specific room, granted by
  the operator as a Pro voucher. No card is needed, the room returns to Free when
  the term ends, and the trial includes no scoring.

Your first room and the free rooms from your tier stay Free. A scoring voucher
cannot be applied to a Free room: scoring needs a Pro room first. When a trial
ends and no subscription follows, the room goes back to Free and its scoring is
hidden, not deleted, until the room is Pro again. A scoring you already paid for
still completes.

---

## When a subscription lapses

Failure to pay never deletes your documents.

1. A failed renewal is retried automatically after 24 and 48 hours.
2. After the last retry the room is **frozen**: investors with existing access can
   still read it, but you cannot grant new access or upload, and the room is
   hidden from the catalog.
3. A cancelled subscription also leaves the room frozen, not deleted. You can
   unfreeze it from the **Subscriptions** page.
4. If the owner is under a sanction or the account is deactivated, charging stops
   and the room freezes until the reason ends.

**Cancelling** happens only in the cabinet, on **Subscriptions**, in at most three
screens, each with a "cancel now" option. The last screen shows a one-time
retention offer of 50% off per room. Inactive members get a warning email three
days before each charge. Account sleep does **not** pause billing, so cancel what
you no longer want before you go quiet.

## Scoring

A scoring is a deterministic evaluation of a project on two axes: **Potential**
(0-150) and **Readiness** (0-150).

- **It starts from the Data Room.** The room collects the project information
  the scoring uses, and ordering needs a Pro room _(rolling out)_.
- **Who can order:** the company's founder of record, from a Pro room.
- **Price:** $35, paid from your payment balance or with a scoring voucher.
- **It does not expire with time.** A scoring stays valid while it was produced by
  the current scoring engine version. It is shown publicly only while the room is
  Pro; otherwise it is hidden, not deleted.
- **Public catalog.** A room is listed with a valid scoring, a completed company
  profile and a Pro room. Without them the room still works for investors you
  granted access to.

### Scoring vouchers

- A voucher is a prepaid scoring. Buy vouchers from your payment balance; a Pro
  subscription includes one per month. Vouchers are valid for three months and
  can be used only in a Pro room; without one, they wait in your wallet until a
  Pro room is available or they expire.
- A top-up of $500 or more gives three **bonus vouchers** _(rolling out)_: valid
  90 days, scoring only, not transferable or withdrawable. See
  [Payouts and rewards](10-payouts-and-rewards.md#topping-up-the-payment-balance).
- A voucher can be passed on **once**, only to the founder of record of a company,
  and only if that person accepts.

## Effects of account status

| Situation | Effect on Data Rooms and scoring |
|---|---|
| **Sleeping account** (30 days without login) | Payments, top-ups, scoring purchases and new rooms are frozen. Existing rooms keep working; subscriptions keep charging. |
| **Revoked KYC** | New grants, new rooms and payouts pause until you verify again. Existing rooms and grants keep working. |
| **Deactivated account** | Public listings are unpublished. Rooms are not deleted. |
| **Deletion at your request** | Rooms you own are removed with the account. |

See [Trust and safety](07-trust-and-safety.md#inactivity-deletion-and-your-data).
