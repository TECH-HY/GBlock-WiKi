# 9. Launch scope and stage

This page says what GBlock includes at public launch, what is still being
switched on, and what is deliberately not planned. It exists so founders,
investors and members can calibrate expectations.

---

## Where GBlock is today

GBlock is in **pre-market testing**, a controlled phase before public launch.
Anyone can register with an email address, while market access is earned through
vouches. Running in this phase does not mean a market release or live commercial
traffic.

This wiki describes the product as designed for launch. Functions that are not
yet visible to every member are marked _rolling out_. Limits and prices are the
current catalog values and can change before launch.

---

## What the launch includes

**Community and reputation**

- Registration by email, welcome grant, invitations and vouch gifts, and the
  choice of paths after the first sign-in.
- The Ambassador path with referral rewards, reward tiers from 10% to 25%,
  multi-level overrides and a pool for top ambassadors.
- Resident status through the waitlist and vouches, plus a one-time stage of
  launch grants for the first Residents; trust levels Anonymous, Vouched and
  Verified; reputation tiers Member to Vanguard.

**Founders and projects**

- Data Rooms with owner-controlled documents, access grants, teammates and
  hand-over. Free and Pro rooms.
- Project scoring, the public project catalog, scoring vouchers and
  subscriptions.
- Silent referrals: every link to a Data Room can bring new members.

**Market**

- The dealflow, sale blocks, access requests and buyer interest.
- The Demand Board and the Secondary Market; primary-market blocks by founders
  of scored companies, with no operator approval step.
- Mediated deal threads with names hidden until both sides agree to disclose
  them, complaints and disputes.

**Wallet and support**

- Reward and payment balances, card top-ups, transfers with a 3% bonus, and
  operator-reviewed payouts in USDT.
- Support tickets, reporting to the operator, and data export and deletion.
- Optional two-factor authentication and sign-in alerts.

## Rolling out

These are confirmed parts of the launch product being switched on in stages. Some
members may already see them; not everyone does yet.

- **Free and Pro Data Rooms.** Scoring, the public catalog, primary-market
  blocks and the AI data check need a Pro room; the first room and a Resident's free rooms stay Free.
  See [Data Rooms, scoring and subscriptions](12-data-rooms-scoring-and-subscriptions.md#free-and-pro-rooms).
- **Top-up bonus vouchers.** A top-up of $500 or more gives three bonus scoring
  vouchers. A refund of topped-up funds carries a 15% refund fee. See
  [Payouts and rewards](10-payouts-and-rewards.md#topping-up-the-payment-balance).
- **Payout limits with KYC.** Up to $1,500 per month, and a 72-hour hold on the
  first payout to a new address.
- **Recovery window** after a penalty or a withdrawn vouch.
- **Following Data Rooms and your gallery,** with a feed, a weekly digest and a
  public alias. See [Silent Data Room referrals](11-silent-dr-referral.md#following-a-data-room-and-your-gallery).
- **Sanctions that follow the person,** not the account, and the handling of
  rewards, bonuses and balance after a permanent ban. See
  [Permanent ban](07-trust-and-safety.md#permanent-ban).
- **Document watermarking** in Data Rooms.
- **Sign-in by a one-time code** from your email, and two-factor authentication
  with backup codes for members who switch it on.
- **Faster reputation growth for Verified members:** +20% with KYC, +30% with KYC
  and two-factor authentication.

## Not at launch

- **English only.** The interface and documents are in English at launch.
- **Office files for recipients.** Word, Excel and PowerPoint files can be
  uploaded, but recipients see PDF. Use PDF for anything you want investors to
  open.
- **Single-use shared links to a room.** Access is by direct grant to named
  members or by the public level the owner chooses.
- **Automatic fees on deals.** GBlock takes no automatic commission on deals
  between members. A fee for an introduction applies only under separate written
  terms and is invoiced by the operator.
- **Availability everywhere.** Countries open in stages; see
  [What is GBlock](01-what-is-gblock.md#who-can-use-gblock).

## Later

Directional, not committed. Do not plan around a date.

- A self-service flow from a Data Room to a scored primary-market listing.
- Paid advisor consultations.
- Integrations with regulated counterparties, subject to jurisdictional limits.

---

## What is out of scope

- **Transactional settlement.** GBlock arranges introductions; it does not move
  money or assets between members.
- **Custody** of assets, funds or securities.
- **Investment advice.** Members do their own diligence.
- **A public marketplace.** Market surfaces are for Residents.
- **Crypto or token products.** A block is a private package, nothing more.
- **Guaranteed returns.** No promise of profit, ever.
