# 1. What is GBlock

GBlock is a **private B2B market club** and the dealflow layer of the TECH HY
Venture Club ecosystem. It is a closed, reputation-governed space where founders
present their companies, investors and owners discover private-market
opportunities, and every introduction is mediated. Anyone can
register with an email address; full market participation (the **Resident**
status) comes through vouches from existing members.

> _Where your reputation becomes your strongest asset._

---

## The idea

Private markets are safer when every participant has something to lose. GBlock
builds that into the product:

1. **Reputation is the currency.** Reputation unlocks higher reward rates and
   the ability to back others. It cannot be bought; it is earned and it can be
   lost.
2. **Access is governed.** The dealflow is open to Residents only. Every
   introduction and every Data Room grant is a deliberate, reviewable action.
3. **Accountability is shared.** People who invite or vouch for others carry part
   of the responsibility for them. That keeps the network honest as it grows.

Two more commitments follow from these:

- **Identity is layered.** You can take part anonymously, or verify your
  identity (KYC) to unlock fuller access and more trust in deals.
- **Introductions are mediated.** Names and contact details stay hidden until
  both sides agree to disclose them, and the conversation runs inside GBlock
  until the contract is signed.

The result is a quieter, more disciplined alternative to open deal boards: a
private terminal, not a public marketplace.

## Who it is for

| You are | What GBlock gives you |
|---|---|
| **A founder** | A Data Room for your company, an independent project scoring, and a place in the public project catalog, with full control over who sees what. |
| **An investor or owner** | Access to private-market opportunities (primary raises, secondary blocks, pre-IPO, OTC, M&A, institutional mandates) and a Demand Board to signal what you are looking for. |
| **An ambassador** | A way to grow the community by invitation and earn rewards from the revenue your network generates. |
| **A guarantor or voucher** | A way to put your reputation behind someone you trust and bring them into the club. |

One account covers all of these. You are not a "buyer account" or a "seller
account"; you are a member who can act in any mode your status and trust level
allow. See [User roles and statuses](02-user-roles-and-statuses.md).

---

## What GBlock is not

GBlock is deliberately narrow.

**GBlock is not:**

- an exchange, broker/dealer, custodian, settlement agent or investment advisor;
- a public securities offering or a public marketplace;
- a crypto, token or blockchain product. A _block_ is a private package of
  assets or an opportunity, nothing more;
- a platform that settles transactions. GBlock arranges introductions; it does
  not move money or assets between members;
- a source of investment returns or profit promises.

**GBlock does not:**

- hold custody of any asset, funds or securities;
- guarantee that an opportunity is available, fairly priced or legal in your
  jurisdiction, or vouch for the accuracy of seller information. Members do their
  own diligence;
- pay rewards from deal volume. Rewards come only from TECH HY's own
  non-transactional revenue (scoring, subscriptions and voucher purchases),
  never from fees on deals between members.

If anyone claims GBlock offers guaranteed profit, risk-free returns, passive
income or instant payouts, it is not from GBlock. Report it to the operator.

---

## Who can use GBlock

GBlock is open to companies and to individuals. Payments and payouts are closed
in countries and regions under comprehensive sanctions. Availability by country
is opened in stages: before live payments start, the list of countries and the
consumer rules that apply to each (for example withdrawal rights and taxes) are
confirmed.
