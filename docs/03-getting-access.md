# 3. Getting access

Anyone can register with an email address, but registration does not drop you
into the dealflow. Full club membership (the **Resident** status) is gated by
sponsorship from current members. This page lists every legitimate path in, how
each one works, and what to do if you do not have an invitation yet.

---

## The paths into GBlock

| Path | Who it is for | What it gets you |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-registration by email** | Anyone, including community builders who want to grow the network | A registered account and the Ambassador path (invitations + referral rewards, no dealflow) |
| **Member invitation** | People invited by an existing member | A registered account, the invite welcome grant (40 reputation + 1 vouch), onboarding |
| **Becoming a Resident** | Members ready to enter dealflow | Resident status (dealflow, blocks, vouching) — through guarantees or the waitlist |
| **Founding operator grant** | The bootstrap cohort, selected by the operator | Resident status, Verified Trust Tier, 14-day probation |
| **Community request** | Anyone without an invitation | A way to ask the community for one |

There is also an informal door: clicking a member's **Data Room link** and
registering. That route attributes you to the member who shared the room
(silently, with no obligations for either side) — see
[Silent Data Room referrals](11-silent-dr-referral.md).

Registration and invitations are free. The first two paths are open to anyone
who can reach them. Becoming a Resident requires you to already be inside
GBlock. The founding grant is operator-driven and not openly requestable. The
community request is the fallback if you have no contact inside.

## Path 1: Self-registration by email

The landing page has a one-page registration form. There is no separate
application and no role to pick at the entry.

**How it works:**

1. Open <https://gblock.club> and enter your work email in the registration
   form.
2. Accept the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
3. You receive a secure link by email, sent from an official gblock.club
   address. Opening it completes your registration. There are no passwords.
4. After your first login you choose your paths in the cabinet. Taking the
   Ambassador path gives you the 30-point starting reputation floor.

**What you get as Ambassador:**

- The right to send invitations (each one reserves 10 of your reputation
  points).
- Referral attribution and reward eligibility when your invitees and
  referrals contribute.
- Read-only access to the Secondary Market and the Demand Board.

**What you do NOT get:**

- Ambassador alone does **not** grant dealflow access. To see blocks, place
  blocks, or enter deal threads you also need to become a Resident. Read the
  [Ambassador Terms](https://gblock.club/ambassador-terms) for the rights and
  obligations of the Ambassador path.

If the email already has an account, the form points you to the member login
instead of creating a second account.

## Path 2: Member invitation

An existing member with the right to invite sends you an invitation by email.

**What happens:**

1. The inviter opens **Send Invite** in their cabinet and enters your email.
   **10 reputation points** of the inviter are reserved as a stake.
2. You receive an email with a magic link, sent from an official gblock.club
   address.
3. You click the link. GBlock verifies the token, consumes it (one-time use),
   and authenticates you.
4. If your profile is incomplete, you are sent to profile completion and then
   to the choice of your paths. If your profile is already complete, you land in
   the cabinet.

**Important details:**

- The magic link is signed, single-use, and expires after 30 minutes. The
  invitation itself stays open for 30 days.
- Login responses are generic on purpose. GBlock does not reveal whether an
  email is already registered, to prevent email enumeration.
- **Welcome grant:** a direct registration starts with 30 reputation. An
  invite-activated registration starts with **40** (the +10 invite bonus on top
  of the 30-point floor) plus **1 vouch gift** — a real vouch that counts toward
  Resident status.
- The inviter's reserved 10 points are returned when your invitation activates
  or expires. If you misbehave, the inviter may face a reputation impact.

## Path 3: Becoming a Resident

If you are already inside GBlock and you want market access, there are two
routes. Both end in the same Resident status.

**Guarantee route.** Choose **Apply for Residency** in your cabinet and request
guarantees on the Guarantee Board. You need **two guarantees from KYC-verified
members**. Each guarantee starts a probation (30 days by default); when it
completes in good standing, you become a Resident. The application only opens
the process: Resident surfaces stay locked until it is completed.

**Waitlist route.** Complete KYC, join the waitlist and collect **two vouches,
at least one of whom is a KYC-verified Resident** (the decisive second vouch
must come from a KYC-verified Resident). An invitation vouch gift counts as a
real vouch. Each vouch credits reputation to both sides: +10 to you, +5 to the
member who vouched for you. Your voucher also stakes part of their reputation
capacity, so vouches are never casual — see
[Reputation economy - Vouches](05-reputation-economy.md#vouches). A full
Resident also gets additional Data Room allowances — see
[Data Rooms, scoring and subscriptions](12-data-rooms-scoring-and-subscriptions.md).

A founding-grant Resident who has completed their 14-day probation can also
act as your (decisive) voucher.

Full mechanics, including what happens when a vouch is reworked, are in
[User journeys - Guarantee and vouch for members](04-user-journeys.md#guarantee-and-vouch-for-members)
and [Trust and safety - Probation](07-trust-and-safety.md#probation).

## Path 4: Founding operator grant

This is a bootstrap path used during the early growth phase. The operator
grants Resident status to a selected member with a recorded reason and
evidence.

- Limited to 50 grants, or until 100 active Residents exist.
- Founding Residents must pass KYC, so they enter as Verified Trust Tier.
- They serve a 14-day probation during which they cannot issue guarantees.
- After probation, they form the initial pool of Verified guarantors.

This path is not openly requestable. It is an operator decision tied to the
club's launch phase.

## Path 5: Community request

If you have no contact inside GBlock, you can also ask the community directly.

1. Open <https://gblock.club> and use the **Request access from the community**
   link.
2. You are taken to the official TECH HY Venture Club community on Telegram:
   <https://t.me/TECH_HY_VC/6>.
3. Introduce yourself and your interest in private markets. If an existing
   member is willing to invite you, they will send you an invitation.

This path keeps Resident membership sponsorship-based while still letting
genuine applicants find a sponsor. It is slower than a warm introduction, but
it is the intended route for applicants who want a sponsor and have no existing
network inside the club.

## What happens after you get in

Regardless of which path brought you in, the first session follows the same
onboarding flow:

1. **Magic link consumption.** Click the link, get authenticated.
2. **Profile completion.** Fill in the details GBlock needs to operate. If your
   profile is already complete, this step is skipped.
3. **Choosing your paths.** Pick one or more: Data Room & Scoring, Ambassador,
   Apply for Residency. They are not mutually exclusive, and you can combine all
   three. Each path asks for its own consent.
4. **Cabinet home.** You arrive at your member cabinet, where you see your
   reputation hero, trust tier badge, tier catalog position, standing, and
   quick actions.

From there, what you can do depends on your status and trust tier. See
[User roles and statuses](02-user-roles-and-statuses.md) and
[User journeys](04-user-journeys.md).

## A note on shared accountability

Every invitation and every guarantee carries shared responsibility. The person
who brought you in stakes reputation on your conduct. If you violate the rules
of the space, both you and your inviter or guarantor face a reputation impact.
This is not a penalty for its own sake; it is the mechanism that lets GBlock
stay reputation-governed without a centralized gatekeeper on every action.

Read the full conduct and enforcement model in
[Trust and safety](07-trust-and-safety.md).
