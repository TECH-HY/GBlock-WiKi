# 13. Legal documents

The documents you agree to or should read when you use GBlock: the terms, the
privacy and cookie policies, the disclaimers and the risk disclosure. This page
says what each one covers and when you meet it, and links to the full text.

> **The texts on gblock.club are the binding versions.** This page is a plain
> guide and does not replace or change them. If this wiki and a legal document
> differ, the legal document applies. The documents are being revised ahead of
> public launch so that they match the launch design described in this guide.
> When a new version is published, you are asked to accept it, and the version
> and date shown on the site are the ones that count.

## Important notices

These points are repeated in the documents below and across this wiki. They are
worth reading before you do anything else.

- **GBlock is not an exchange, broker, custodian, settlement agent or investment
  advisor.** It arranges introductions. It holds no assets, moves no deal funds
  and gives no investment, legal or tax advice. See
  [What GBlock is not](01-what-is-gblock.md#what-gblock-is-not).
- **No promise of returns.** Private-market investments carry real risk, up to the
  loss of everything invested, and they can be hard to sell. Nothing on GBlock is
  a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Do your own diligence.
- **Information comes from the people who publish it.** What a Data Room or a
  scoring contains comes from its owner and GBlock does not verify it. See
  [Data Rooms, scoring and subscriptions](12-data-rooms-scoring-and-subscriptions.md#what-you-publish-is-your-responsibility).
- **A scoring is an information product, not advice.** It is not an investment
  recommendation, a valuation, a rating or a guarantee of fundraising, success or
  return, and it is only as good as the inputs it was given.
- **Availability depends on your country.** Payments and payouts are closed in
  countries and regions under comprehensive sanctions. See
  [Who can use GBlock](01-what-is-gblock.md#who-can-use-gblock).

## How acceptance works

- **Registration.** You accept the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy before
  your account is created. Optional marketing is a separate choice and never
  blocks registration.
- **Payments.** A payment screen links the documents that apply, such as the
  Terms and the Payments, Refunds and Cancellation Terms, and a scoring purchase
  adds the Scoring Service Offer. You accept them with a checkbox before you pay.
- **Data Rooms.** Before the first access to a restricted room, you confirm the
  confidentiality terms of that room.
- **Updates.** When the Terms or the Privacy Policy change in a material way,
  you are asked to accept the new version, with a grace period of 14 days to do
  so.
- **Records.** GBlock keeps a record of what you accepted, which version and
  when.
