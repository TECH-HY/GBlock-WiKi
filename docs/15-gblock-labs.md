# 15. GBlock Labs

## What Labs is

GBlock Labs is a venture studio inside GBlock. It takes a project from first
assessment to a deal-ready company, with AI speed and a human sign-off at every
stage.

---

## Who it is for

Labs is for project teams that have registered on GBlock and run self-scoring.

---

## How to start

1. **Register on GBlock** with the single form. Every member starts as an
   Ambassador, and no invitation is needed. See
   [Getting access](03-getting-access.md).
2. **Run self-scoring** from your cabinet. See
   [Data Rooms, scoring and subscriptions](12-data-rooms-scoring-and-subscriptions.md).
3. When you create the project's Data Room as its founder, tick the
   **Labs partnership interest** _(rolling out)_.

GBlock reviews the projects that opt in.

---

## The six stages

1. **Intro & assessment.** The Labs team meets the project team and assesses fit.
2. **Scoring.** The project completes GBlock scoring based on its Data Room.
3. **Expert analysis & audit.** Experts review the scoring, product and market.
4. **Offer.** Labs makes a written offer describing scope, team and terms.
5. **Agreement with strategic partners.** The team and strategic partners agree
   before the build starts.
6. **Build, verification & packaging.** Labs builds, verifies and packages the
   product and company; the project is introduced to the club as deal-ready.

---

## What the subscription covers

The subscription includes a project manager, a domain expert, build and
verification, design and packaging, partner introductions and club networking.

Labs works under a monthly subscription; the commercial terms are agreed
individually for each project.

---

## AI and people

AI supports research, drafts, decks, MVP code and Data Room assembly. People
select and own every gate, make the club introductions, and verify work before
anything is published.

---

## Labs and scoring

Scoring is an assessment of a project. Labs is hands-on building after an offer
is agreed, with partners supporting the work toward a deal-ready company.

---
