# SCHEDULE 8 - COOKIE POLICY

## 1. Scope

This Schedule explains how GBlock uses cookies and similar storage or access technologies. It should be read with Schedule 7.

## 2. Strictly necessary technologies

GBlock may use strictly necessary technologies for:

(a) login and authenticated sessions;
(b) security and fraud prevention;
(c) rate limiting and session integrity;
(d) load balancing and service continuity;
(e) remembering privacy choices; and
(f) other core technical functions.

Where applicable law permits these technologies without consent, they may be used on that basis. Blocking them may prevent core functions from working.

## 3. Analytics technologies

Where consent is required, optional analytics technologies are enabled only after the User opts in. They may help GBlock understand feature use, errors, performance, and aggregate service behaviour.

GBlock does not use optional analytics to build third-party cross-site behavioural advertising profiles.

## 4. Current cookie register

The cookie settings interface or banner should provide the current operational register of non-essential technologies, including provider, purpose, and duration or expiry where applicable. Technical names may change as infrastructure changes.

## 5. Third-party technologies

A third-party provider may set or access a technology when providing an integrated Platform function. Where consent is required, non-essential technologies will not be enabled before the relevant consent is obtained.

## 6. Managing choices

Users may accept or reject optional technologies through the cookie interface and may change choices later. Browser-level controls may also be used but may impair core Platform functions.

## 7. No behavioural advertising network

Under the current service model, GBlock does not permit third-party advertising networks to use the Platform for cross-site behavioural advertising and does not sell cookie-derived personal data.

## 8. Changes

This Schedule may be updated as technology or law changes. The cookie settings interface is the current operational source for specific cookie details.
