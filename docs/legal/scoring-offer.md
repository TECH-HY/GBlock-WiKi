2.1 You must be at least 18, have legal capacity and be the company's owner or its authorised representative acting through the permitted founder purchase flow. ## 3. The service and delivery

3.1 GBlock automatically applies a versioned scoring methodology to information you provide, including the questionnaire, metrics and Data Room inputs. Following successful payment or Voucher redemption the report is generated automatically. This is not a purchase of a manual analyst review, independent due diligence, a regulated rating or a professional opinion.

3.2 The report contains two-axis assessments, an overall score, grade and quadrant, dimensional analysis, strengths and risks, a roadmap, a financial summary, a fundraising scenario and a version suitable for publication. These are informational outputs, not promises that the underlying claims have been independently verified or that funding will occur.

3.3 The report is private by default. The founder decides whether to publish it. It describes the input snapshot and methodology version at generation, rather than continuously monitored information. It has no contractual expiry solely because 30 days pass. Applicable correction, withdrawal and payment-reversal provisions still apply.

3.4 You may buy another scoring at any time, including on the same day, at the same current price. The new report replaces the previous one. There is no automatic scoring renewal and no promise of a particular improvement in score.

3.5 Scoring does not assure sanctions clearance, intellectual-property rights or ownership validity. No external AI processing or human review is promised by this Offer. Schedule 2 and Section 18 of the Master Platform Terms describe the service limitations.

4.1 The price is USD 35.00 for one scoring service, whether first or repeated. The listed scoring price is fixed for the accepted purchase and has no separate repeat-purchase discount. The payment screen must show the matching price and USD currency before confirmation. A price change requires an updated Offer and applies only to future purchases.

4.2 The USD 35.00 price excludes applicable taxes. Taxes required by law may be added and will be disclosed before you confirm payment. Tax handling must match Section 2.3 of Schedule 4 and Section 21.5 of the Master Platform Terms.

4.3 You may fund the purchase with Payment balance, a card through Stripe Checkout, or a valid purchased or operator-issued Voucher. Card Checkout accepts cards; no other external payment rail is promised. A Voucher covers one service, expires three months from issue and has no cash redemption value.

4.4 Where Payment balance is used, the USD 35.00 scoring price is debited, together with any applicable taxes disclosed before payment. A valid Voucher settles the service entitlement without an additional USD 35.00 card or balance debit. Top-up bonuses and the reward-transfer uplift add service credit and do not change the listed scoring price. A monthly Subscription Voucher or operator-issued Voucher is not a general offer of free scoring to all Users.

4.5 For the identified technical failures, the payment correction rules in Section 5.2 of Schedule 4 apply. In particular, a balance-funded scoring transaction rolls back its debit if report generation fails. No universal automatic refund or card-refund timing is promised.
