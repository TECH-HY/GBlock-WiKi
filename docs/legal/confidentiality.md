# SCHEDULE 3 - DATA ROOM CONFIDENTIALITY TERMS

## 1. Formation of Data Room confidentiality agreement

1.1 When a recipient is presented with a Data Room access screen identifying the Data Room Owner and clicks an acceptance action such as "I Agree" or "Access Data Room", the recipient agrees to this Schedule in relation to that Data Room.

1.2 To the extent permitted by applicable law and the access screen, the Data Room Owner is an express beneficiary of and may enforce the recipient's confidentiality and restricted-use obligations in this Schedule. TECH HY may also enforce these obligations as Platform operator and to protect Platform integrity.

1.3 If a separately accepted NDA applies to the same Data Room, the more specific or stricter confidentiality obligation prevails to the extent of conflict unless that NDA expressly states otherwise.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" includes all non-public information made available in or through a Data Room or otherwise disclosed in connection with Data Room access, including:

(a) financial statements, management accounts, forecasts, budgets, models, and tax information;
(b) cap tables, shareholder information, securities information, financing documents, valuations, and transaction materials;
(c) customer, supplier, employee, contractor, partner, pipeline, pricing, and commercial information;
(d) product roadmaps, source code, architecture, technical information, security information, intellectual property, research, designs, and know-how;
(e) legal documents, litigation information, regulatory information, licences, compliance materials, and board materials;
(f) personal data and customer information;
(g) the fact that particular non-public materials or discussions exist; and
(h) notes, extracts, analyses, summaries, screenshots, models, or other materials derived from the foregoing.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the recipient can demonstrate with contemporaneous evidence:

(a) was lawfully public without breach of this Schedule;
(b) was lawfully known to the recipient without confidentiality restriction before disclosure;
(c) was lawfully obtained from a third party not known by the recipient to be under a confidentiality duty; or
(d) was independently developed without use of Confidential Information.

The burden of demonstrating an exclusion rests on the recipient relying on it.

## 4. Permitted purpose

The recipient may use Confidential Information only to evaluate, discuss, or conduct authorised diligence concerning the Data Room Owner or a potential relationship or transaction with it (the "Permitted Purpose").

No other licence or right is granted by disclosure.

## 5. Core restrictions

Unless the Data Room Owner expressly permits otherwise in writing or through Platform controls, the recipient must not:

(a) disclose, forward, publish, post, distribute, or make Confidential Information available to another person;
(b) download, copy, photograph, screen-record, screenshot, scrape, extract, OCR, mirror, or systematically reproduce materials where the Platform disables or restricts that activity;
(c) use Confidential Information to compete unfairly with the Data Room Owner;
(d) use Confidential Information to contact, solicit, target, or interfere with customers, employees, contractors, suppliers, or partners in a manner unrelated to the Permitted Purpose or otherwise improper;
(e) use Confidential Information for trading, market abuse, unlawful securities activity, or another unlawful purpose;
(f) remove watermarks, access controls, confidentiality notices, document identifiers, or security markings;
(g) attempt to discover information beyond the recipient's authorised permission level;
(h) upload Confidential Information into a public or third-party generative AI, machine-learning, transcription, summarisation, document-analysis, or data-processing service unless the Data Room Owner has expressly authorised that use and the service is permitted under applicable confidentiality and privacy obligations; or
(i) use Confidential Information to train a model, dataset, or external knowledge base without express written permission.

## 6. Permitted representatives

6.1 The recipient may disclose Confidential Information to directors, employees, partners, legal advisers, tax advisers, accountants, auditors, investment committee members, and other professional advisers who:

(a) reasonably need the information for the Permitted Purpose; and
(b) are bound by professional, contractual, fiduciary, or employment confidentiality obligations at least sufficient to protect the information.

6.2 The recipient remains responsible for a permitted representative's use or disclosure to the extent the recipient caused, authorised, or failed to take reasonable steps to prevent a breach.

6.3 The recipient must not share login credentials or permit a representative to use the recipient's account where GBlock requires the representative to have a separate authorised account.

## 7. Compelled disclosure

If disclosure is required by law, regulation, court, regulator, stock exchange, or binding governmental order, the recipient may disclose only the portion legally required. Where legally permitted and reasonably practicable, the recipient must give the Data Room Owner prompt notice before disclosure so the Data Room Owner may seek protective relief.

## 8. Security duties

The recipient must:

(a) use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards;
(b) limit access to authorised persons;
(c) protect credentials and devices;
(d) not store permitted copies in insecure public locations; and
(e) notify legal@gblock.club promptly after becoming aware of suspected unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure involving Data Room materials.

## 9. Access logging and evidence

9.1 The recipient acknowledges that GBlock may record Data Room access, views, downloads where enabled, permission changes, device or session information, and security events.

9.2 GBlock may share relevant access records with the Data Room Owner, advisers, insurers, authorities, or courts where reasonably necessary to investigate or enforce a suspected breach and where permitted by privacy law.

## 10. No representation or warranty by disclosure

10.1 Confidential Information is supplied for evaluation and may be incomplete, unaudited, preliminary, forward-looking, or subject to change.

10.2 Neither TECH HY nor the Data Room Owner makes a warranty merely by providing access that the materials contain all information material to a decision.

10.3 The recipient must conduct its own diligence and rely only on representations and warranties contained in definitive transaction documents, if any.

## 11. No obligation to transact

Access to a Data Room does not oblige the Data Room Owner, TECH HY, or the recipient to continue discussions, disclose additional information, negotiate, or complete a transaction.

## 12. Return and deletion

12.1 When access ends or the Data Room Owner reasonably requests it, the recipient must stop using Confidential Information.

12.2 Where reasonably practicable, the recipient must delete or destroy copies lawfully made, except copies retained because of law, professional obligations, bona fide internal compliance records, legal hold, or automated backups not reasonably capable of selective deletion.

12.3 Any retained copy remains subject to this Schedule and may not be used for another purpose.

## 13. Duration

13.1 These confidentiality and restricted-use obligations continue for five years after the recipient's last access to the relevant Data Room.

13.2 For information qualifying as a trade secret or receiving longer protection under applicable law, the confidentiality and restricted-use obligations continue for so long as that information retains its protected character, unless the Data Room Owner releases the recipient in writing.

## 14. Remedies

14.1 The recipient acknowledges that unauthorised disclosure or misuse may cause harm that is difficult to quantify.

14.2 TECH HY and, where enforceable, the Data Room Owner may seek damages, injunctions, preservation orders, specific relief, account restriction, or other remedies available under applicable law.

14.3 This Schedule does not create a remedy that applicable law does not permit.
