## 1. Scope and definitions

1.1 This Schedule applies to scoring, Payment balance top-ups, automatic top-ups, paid Data Room subscriptions and scoring vouchers. It forms part of the [Master Platform Terms](https://gblock.club/legal/terms).

1.2 A Scoring Report is the automated informational output described in [Schedule 2](https://gblock.club/legal/scoring-disclaimer) and the [Scoring Service Offer](https://gblock.club/legal/scoring-offer). A Subscription is the monthly paid service for a Data Room. A Voucher is a prepaid entitlement to one scoring service. Customer Service is GBlock's support team, not a separate contracting party.

1.3 Payment balance is prepayment for eligible GBlock services; it is not withdrawable and earns no interest. Reward balance comprises referral rewards governed by [Schedule 5](https://gblock.club/legal/referral-terms). A Top-up bonus is additional service credit, has no cash value and is not a withdrawable reward.

## 2. Prices and taxes

2.1 One scoring service costs USD 35.00, whether it is your first or a later purchase. Each paid Data Room Subscription costs USD 35.00 per month. A purchased Voucher costs the applicable scoring price. There is no separate price category for repeat scoring.

2.2 The price, currency, service description and payment method are disclosed before payment. The price accepted on the payment screen governs that purchase. Changes apply to future purchases only. A change to an existing Subscription's price requires 30 days' advance notice.

2.3 The USD 35.00 price excludes applicable taxes. Taxes required by law may be added and will be disclosed before you confirm payment. You remain responsible for taxes you are legally required to bear. GBlock may collect, withhold or report taxes where required by law.

## 3. Final payments and mandatory rights

3.1 All payments are final and non-refundable, subject to rights and remedies that applicable law does not permit to be excluded. GBlock provides no self-service refund facility. If you dispute a payment or service, contact Customer Service; each case is reviewed individually and manually. Contacting Customer Service does not itself create a contractual entitlement to a refund.

3.2 In particular, changing your mind, not using the service, cancelling during a paid Subscription period, account closure or restriction, disagreement with a score, an unused Payment balance or Voucher, or a later price change does not by itself entitle you to a refund. There is no contractual cooling-off or satisfaction guarantee and no contractual refund entitlement before performance begins. Mandatory rights remain unaffected.

3.3 This policy does not waive statutory, card-scheme or unauthorised-payment rights. You may raise a mandatory-law claim through Customer Service without giving up any available court, regulator or bank remedy.

## 4. Customer Service and individual manual review

4.1 Contact Customer Service using the Support form in your account and support@gblock.club. Provide the relevant payment reference and an explanation without sending full card details. Legal notices use the separate legal-notice channel in Section 37.1 of the Master Platform Terms.

4.2 Customer Service acknowledges receipt within 24 hours and gives a reasoned decision within 72 hours. These are shared parameters of this Schedule; references in other documents do not establish different deadlines.

4.3 Possible outcomes of manual review are correction of a payment error, an individually approved refund to the original payment method, or refusal with reasons. This is a review procedure, not an advance promise of an exception or refund on request. An approved card refund is processed manually by an operator through Stripe. Bank, network and settlement times are outside GBlock's direct control; no card-credit date is promised. A discretionary credit to Payment balance is not offered as a review outcome.

## 5. Scoring payments and technical errors

5.1 Scoring is purchased separately each time. It requires an active Data Room and is delivered automatically after successful payment or valid Voucher redemption. It does not automatically renew. A low score or dissatisfaction with the result does not create a refund entitlement, subject always to mandatory law.

5.2 Where a balance-funded scoring transaction fails to generate its report, the transaction rolls back the related debit. Where a first Data Room payment fails to become a created room, the applicable first-payment recovery process restores the service credit to Payment balance. These are corrections for those identified technical failures, not customer-requested refunds or a promise of an automatic remedy for every service failure. For other errors, contact Customer Service.

## 6. Payment balance, top-ups and bonuses

6.1 Manual top-ups range from USD 10.00 to USD 100,000.00. They buy service prepayment, not a deposit for withdrawal. Unused Payment balance and Top-up bonuses are not redeemable for cash or returnable on request, subject to mandatory rights and individual manual review under Section 4.

6.2 The current manual top-up bonus is 5% for amounts above USD 150.00 and below USD 500.00, and 10% for amounts of USD 500.00 or more. No tier bonus applies at exactly USD 150.00. Bonuses are service credit and do not change the listed price of scoring.

6.3 Transfer of eligible Reward balance to Payment balance carries a 3% service-credit uplift, subject to Schedule 5 and applicable verification limits. Once transferred, Payment balance is subject to its non-withdrawable service-credit rules.

## 7. Monthly Data Room Subscriptions

7.1 With your separate express recurring-payment consent, a Subscription is charged monthly, first from Payment balance and then from the saved card. The disclosed arrangement continues until cancellation or applicable suspension. It includes a monthly scoring Voucher. Account inactivity or sleep does not by itself cancel the Subscription.

7.2 You may cancel in your account at any time. Cancellation takes effect immediately; the Data Room is frozen rather than deleted. There is no contractual refund or pro-rata recalculation for the current period, subject to mandatory rights. The cancellation flow may show an offer of 50% off the next Subscription; that is separate from the scoring price and does not prevent cancellation.

7.3 Failed recurring charges are retried after 24 and 48 hours, after which the room freezes; subsequent retries occur every seven days, at most four times. The three-day pre-charge warning applies to inactive accounts, currently those without a sign-in for 14 days; it is not promised to every account.

7.4 Recurring charges are suspended during an open bank dispute and pending operator review of a lost dispute as described in Section 22.6 of the Master Platform Terms. An individual card purchase remains distinct from a recurring charge.

## 8. Automatic top-ups

8.1 Automatic top-ups require separate consent. You choose the trigger balance, top-up amount and monthly limit and may revoke the arrangement. The current limit is three automatic charges in a rolling 24-hour period. The automatic top-up bonus is 1%; it does not stack with the manual tier bonus, and the higher applicable bonus is used.

8.2 Automatic top-ups are suspended during an open bank dispute and pending review of a lost dispute, as described in Section 22.6 of the Master Platform Terms. This Schedule does not state that existing recurring-consent wording contains a no-refund acknowledgement.

## 9. Vouchers

9.1 A Voucher covers one scoring service, expires three months after issue, is not accumulated into a cash balance and has no cash redemption value. It may be transferred once to a company's founder with that recipient's consent. GBlock may issue a Voucher without charge. Redemption settles the scoring entitlement; it does not also debit USD 35.00 from your balance or card.

9.2 An unused Voucher linked to a reversed payment may be revoked. A spent Voucher is not recalled; its value remains payable as debt where applicable. Referral reward adjustments for spent Vouchers and balance-funded Subscription cycles may require manual processing before the first reward payout; this Schedule does not promise automatic clawback for those cases.

## 10. Reversals, bank disputes and debt

10.1 The consequences for rewards, balances, bonuses, Vouchers, Subscriptions, reports and debt are governed by Section 22.4 of the [Master Platform Terms](https://gblock.club/legal/terms). Those consequences apply when an operator issues a refund through Stripe or the bank reverses a payment; processing a reversal does not create a self-service refund right.

10.2 In particular, a full reversal or lost dispute on a direct card payment for scoring invalidates the associated report. A partial reversal does not by itself invalidate it. Reversal of a top-up already spent on scoring leaves the report in place and leaves any shortfall as debt. Applicable bonus and reward adjustments follow Section 22.4.

10.3 You are encouraged to contact Customer Service first, but doing so is not a condition of exercising a lawful bank-dispute right. A lost dispute opens individual operator review under Section 22.6; it does not itself cause an automatic ban. Debt remains until settled through a top-up or eligible rewards, or written off by an operator.

## 11. Account closure with a balance

11.1 Where you request deletion with a positive Payment balance, an operator reviews that balance within 14 calendar days from the request and communicates the decision with reasons. This is a decision deadline, not a guarantee of deletion, refund or cash redemption. If the operator has not decided by that deadline, your request remains pending and is escalated for operator attention. Missing the deadline does not trigger an automatic refund, write-off or anonymised retention of the balance.

11.2 You arrange disposal of Reward balance through an eligible payout request or transfer to Payment balance, subject to the latter's service-credit rules. Customer Service cannot promise to zero or cash out Reward balance on your behalf.

11.3 Active deals, unpaid payouts, legal holds, active sanctions, debt, open disputes and outstanding balances can delay deletion. The ordinary 30-day processing target is not an unconditional deletion guarantee for deferred requests. Mandatory data rights remain unaffected. Section 26.5 of the Master Platform Terms applies.

## 12. Payment processing and delivery evidence

12.1 Payments may be processed by Stripe or another payment provider. GBlock may receive payment status, transaction identifiers, limited payment metadata and billing information but does not store full card numbers where the provider processes them directly.

12.2 GBlock may retain payment, request, scoring-commencement, generation, availability, download or view records and related communications as evidence of delivery, subject to applicable privacy law. GBlock also records, each time you submit a purchase or payment, the documents and versions you accepted at that step, with the time of acceptance, your IP address and browser details. This record evidences acceptance at submission, not completion of the payment.
