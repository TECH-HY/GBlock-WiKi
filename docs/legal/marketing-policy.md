# SCHEDULE 9 - EMAIL AND MARKETING COMMUNICATIONS POLICY

## 1. Necessary communications

GBlock may send communications reasonably necessary to operate, secure, administer, or legally manage an account or service, including:

(a) payment receipts, invoices, and refunds;
(b) login alerts and password resets;
(c) security incidents;
(d) KYC, compliance, sanctions, or verification requests;
(e) account enforcement and appeals;
(f) Data Room access or service-status messages;
(g) service delivery communications;
(h) legal, privacy, policy, and contractual notices; and
(i) other communications required to perform a requested service.

Necessary service communications are not optional marketing merely because they are sent by email.

## 2. Optional marketing

Marketing may include product news, feature announcements, events, community updates, educational digests, and Referral Program promotions.

Creating an account does not by itself subscribe a User to optional marketing.

## 3. Consent

Where marketing relies on consent, the opt-in will be a separate affirmative choice and will not be preselected where law prohibits preselected consent.

TECH HY may retain records showing when, how, and to which marketing categories a User subscribed.

## 4. Unsubscribe

Marketing emails will provide an unsubscribe mechanism. Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop necessary service, security, legal, compliance, or billing messages.

TECH HY may maintain a suppression record to ensure that an unsubscribed address is not inadvertently re-added.

## 5. Frequency

GBlock applies reasonable frequency controls to marketing. Necessary operational, security, legal, and billing messages are excluded from marketing frequency limits.

## 6. Identification

Marketing messages will identify GBlock or TECH HY as sender and provide a functional method to manage marketing preferences.

## 7. No disguised marketing

GBlock will not classify a primarily promotional communication as a security, legal, or billing communication merely to avoid applicable marketing rules.
