# SCHEDULE 7 - PRIVACY POLICY

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how TECH HY SDN. BHD., operating GBlock, collects, uses, shares, stores, and protects personal data. It is intended to address the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010, as amended ("Malaysia PDPA"), and other privacy laws where they apply, including the EU GDPR and UK GDPR.

## 2. Controller and processor roles

2.1 TECH HY generally acts as data controller for account administration, billing, Platform security, access management, scoring operations, referrals, communications, marketing preferences, compliance, and other processing where TECH HY determines purposes and means.

2.2 A Company User may upload personal data to a Data Room. Where TECH HY processes that data solely on the Company User's documented instructions and does not determine the underlying purpose, TECH HY may act as processor and the Company User may remain controller. Additional data processing terms may apply.

2.3 Privacy contact: privacy@gblock.club.

2.4 Where applicable law requires TECH HY to appoint or register a Data Protection Officer, TECH HY will do so and will make appropriate contact details available.

## 3. Categories of personal data

Depending on use of GBlock, TECH HY may process:

(a) account data: name, email, credentials, organisation, role, account identifiers, registration details;
(b) profile data: issuer, investor, professional, employment, company, biography, preferences, or other information a User chooses to provide;
(c) company and scoring data: company information, metrics, documents, evidence, responses, report inputs, and related metadata;
(d) Data Room data: files, metadata, permissions, access history, and personal data contained in uploaded materials;
(e) identity and compliance data: KYC information, identity documents or verification results, sanctions screening, fraud indicators, and compliance records;
(f) payment and billing data: fee amount, transaction reference, invoice, refund, payment status, limited payment metadata, billing details;
(g) referral and payout data: referral code, referred-user relationship, reward calculation, payout history, wallet address or digital-asset transaction information where applicable;
(h) technical and security data: IP address, device, browser, authentication events, login history, session information, access logs, abuse signals, security events;
(i) usage and analytics data: pages, actions, feature usage, errors, and analytics information where permitted and, where required, consented to;
(j) communications: support requests, complaints, correspondence, survey responses, calls or meeting notes where lawfully recorded or documented;
(k) acceptance and consent records: version, timestamp, checkbox, action, session, notice wording, marketing preference, cookie choice, NDA acceptance, and related audit trail; and
(l) other information a User voluntarily supplies for a permitted Platform purpose.

## 4. Sources of personal data

TECH HY may collect personal data:

(a) directly from the individual;
(b) from the organisation or Company User the individual represents;
(c) from other Users where they are authorised to provide it;
(d) from payment, identity, communications, security, hosting, or other service providers;
(e) from public company, professional, regulatory, or business sources where lawful; and
(f) automatically through Platform use and permitted cookies or similar technologies.

## 5. Purposes of processing

TECH HY may process personal data to:

(a) create, authenticate, administer, and secure accounts;
(b) provide profiles, Data Rooms, scoring, introductions, referrals, support, and other requested services;
(c) process payments, refunds, renewals, invoices, and eligible referral payouts;
(d) generate, correct, maintain, and evidence Scoring Reports;
(e) administer and enforce Data Room access and confidentiality;
(f) verify identity, account authority, eligibility, sanctions status, and fraud risk;
(g) prevent abuse, cyber threats, illegal activity, and breaches of this Agreement;
(h) communicate service, legal, billing, security, compliance, and account information;
(i) send marketing where valid consent or another lawful basis permits;
(j) maintain evidence of contracts, consents, actions, and disputes;
(k) comply with tax, accounting, legal, court, law-enforcement, and regulatory obligations;
(l) manage corporate transactions, insurance, audits, professional advice, and claims;
(m) improve Platform reliability, product design, and aggregate analytics; and
(n) protect TECH HY, Users, Data Room Owners, and third parties.

## 6. Legal bases where EU or UK law applies

Depending on the processing, TECH HY may rely on:

(a) performance of a contract or steps requested before entering a contract;
(b) compliance with legal obligations;
(c) legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention, service improvement, dispute handling, business administration, and protection of rights, balanced against individual rights;
(d) consent, including for optional marketing or non-essential cookies where required; and
(e) establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims or other lawful bases where applicable.

Where consent is relied on, it may be withdrawn for future processing without affecting processing already lawfully undertaken.

## 7. Malaysia PDPA compliance

Where the Malaysia PDPA applies, TECH HY will process personal data in accordance with applicable notice, choice, disclosure, security, retention, data integrity, access, data portability, breach notification, processor security, DPO, and cross-border transfer requirements.

## 8. Public and shared profile information

Information a User deliberately publishes on a public or shared profile may be visible to other Users or the public according to the controls shown. A User should not publish personal data unless comfortable with that level of visibility.

## 9. Data Room personal data

9.1 Data Room materials may contain personal data relating to employees, shareholders, customers, suppliers, advisers, or other people.

9.2 Company Users are responsible for ensuring they have a lawful basis and authority to upload and disclose such data.

9.3 TECH HY may log and disclose Data Room access records as described in Schedule 3 and this Privacy Policy.

## 10. Recipients and service providers

TECH HY may disclose personal data to:

(a) cloud, hosting, database, security, authentication, analytics, communications, support, and infrastructure providers;
(b) Stripe or other payment processors;
(c) identity, fraud, sanctions, KYC, tax, payout, or digital-asset service providers where relevant;
(d) professional advisers, auditors, insurers, banks, and consultants under appropriate obligations;
(e) Data Room Owners where access records or suspected breaches reasonably require disclosure;
(f) affiliates or group companies where necessary and lawful;
(g) a genuine buyer, investor, lender, acquirer, successor, or adviser in a corporate transaction, subject to safeguards; and
(h) courts, regulators, law enforcement, tax authorities, government bodies, or others where legally required or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

TECH HY does not sell personal data and does not disclose personal data to third parties for their own unrelated behavioural advertising.

## 11. International transfers

11.1 GBlock is operated from Malaysia and may use providers or infrastructure in other countries.

11.2 Where personal data is transferred outside Malaysia, TECH HY will use a transfer condition or mechanism permitted by section 129 of the Malaysia PDPA and applicable regulatory guidance and will maintain transfer records where required.

11.3 Where EU or UK personal data is transferred internationally and safeguards are legally required, TECH HY may use adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or another permitted mechanism, together with supplementary safeguards where appropriate.

## 12. Data retention

TECH HY retains personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose and applicable legal obligations. Typical principles include:

(a) account and profile information while the account is active and, where justified, up to three years after closure for disputes, security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations;
(b) tax, accounting, invoice, and payment records for legally required periods and, after an account is erased, invoices, wallet transactions and deal records for six years in a form separated from the person's name and identifiers;
(c) acceptance and contract records for the period reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims;
(d) security and Data Room logs for a period proportionate to security, confidentiality, and dispute risks, with longer retention during incidents, investigations, or legal holds;
(e) marketing consent and suppression records for as long as needed to demonstrate and honour communication choices;
(f) KYC and compliance records for legally or operationally justified periods, subject to provider and regulatory requirements; and
(g) Data Room content according to account settings, Company User instructions, contracts, legal holds, and applicable law.

When retention is no longer justified, data is deleted, securely destroyed, or anonymised.

## 13. Security

TECH HY uses technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature and risk of processing, which may include encryption in transit, access control, least privilege, authentication, logging, monitoring, backups, incident response, vendor controls, and secure development or operational practices.

No online system is completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting credentials and devices.

## 14. Personal data breaches

14.1 TECH HY maintains processes to identify, investigate, contain, document, and respond to personal data breaches.

14.2 Where the Malaysia PDPA requires notification to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner, TECH HY will notify in the form and period required by applicable law and guidance.

14.3 Where a breach causes or is likely to cause significant harm and data-subject notification is required, TECH HY will provide notification without unnecessary delay as required by law.

14.4 Other jurisdictions may impose additional notification obligations, which TECH HY will address where applicable.

## 15. Data Protection Officer

15.1 Where TECH HY meets an applicable legal threshold requiring a Data Protection Officer, TECH HY will appoint the required DPO and complete required notification or registration.

15.2 DPO or privacy contact information will be made available through privacy@gblock.club or an updated privacy notice.

## 16. Individual rights

Depending on applicable law, an individual may have rights to:

(a) access personal data;
(b) correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
(c) request deletion or erasure;
(d) restrict processing;
(e) object to certain processing;
(f) withdraw consent;
(g) receive or transmit portable data where a portability right applies;
(h) complain to a competent regulator; and
(i) receive information about certain automated processing where law requires it.

Rights may be limited by legal exceptions, competing rights, confidentiality, legal privilege, fraud prevention, security, or record-keeping obligations.

Requests may be sent to privacy@gblock.club. TECH HY may verify identity before fulfilling a request.

## 17. EU and UK representative

If EU GDPR or UK GDPR requires TECH HY to appoint a local representative, TECH HY will publish the representative's contact details in the current Privacy Policy or relevant regional notice.

## 18. Automated processing and scoring

GBlock scoring primarily evaluates company information. TECH HY does not intend a company score by itself to constitute a solely automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects on an individual. If a future feature materially changes this position, TECH HY will provide additional notice where required.

## 19. Children

GBlock is intended for persons aged 18 and over. TECH HY does not knowingly offer account services to children.

## 20. Marketing privacy

Marketing choices are governed by Schedule 9. Creating an account does not by itself opt a User into optional marketing.

## 21. Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies are governed by Schedule 8.

## 22. Corporate transactions

If TECH HY is involved in a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganisation, sale of assets, or similar transaction, personal data may be disclosed to relevant parties and advisers subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.

## 23. Changes to this Privacy Policy

TECH HY may update this Policy to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes. Material changes will be notified as required by law. Where a new purpose requires consent, TECH HY will seek consent rather than relying solely on a general policy update.

## 24. Complaints

Privacy complaints may be sent to privacy@gblock.club. Individuals may also complain to a competent data protection authority where applicable.
