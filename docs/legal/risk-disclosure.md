# SCHEDULE 1 - RISK DISCLOSURE

## 1. Purpose

This Risk Disclosure describes material risks of early-stage and private-company opportunities that may be visible through GBlock. It is not exhaustive and does not replace professional advice.

## 2. Total loss risk

Early-stage companies frequently fail. An Investor User may lose 100% of invested capital. Private securities are not bank deposits and may not be protected by deposit insurance, investor compensation schemes, or any capital guarantee.

## 3. Illiquidity and transfer restrictions

Private-company securities may have no active market. Shareholders agreements, company constitutions, securities laws, rights of first refusal, board approvals, pre-emption rights, lockups, legends, or contractual restrictions may prevent or delay a transfer. An Investor User may be unable to sell for years or indefinitely.

## 4. Dilution

Future funding rounds, employee option pools, convertibles, SAFEs, warrants, down rounds, restructurings, recapitalisations, anti-dilution provisions, and other instruments may materially dilute an Investor User's ownership or economic rights.

## 5. Valuation uncertainty

Private-company valuations may be subjective and may be based on limited information, negotiation dynamics, internal estimates, last-round prices, comparable-company assumptions, or models that later prove wrong. A prior valuation does not establish current fair value or an achievable exit price.

## 6. Information risk

Company information may be unaudited, incomplete, forward-looking, stale, prepared by management, selectively presented, or incorrect. GBlock does not independently verify every claim.

## 7. Issuer and key-person risk

A private company may depend heavily on issuers, executives, key employees, contractors, intellectual property, licences, or counterparties. Departure, misconduct, illness, conflict, litigation, or loss of key relationships may materially impair the business.

## 8. Product, technology and cybersecurity risk

Products may fail to reach product-market fit, may contain defects, may be displaced by competitors, or may depend on third-party technology. Cyber incidents, data breaches, infrastructure failures, intellectual property disputes, or regulatory changes may materially affect value.

## 9. Revenue, customer and concentration risk

Pipeline is not revenue. Letters of intent, pilots, memoranda of understanding, partnerships, waitlists, or verbal commitments may not convert into revenue. A company may depend on a small number of customers, suppliers, channels, or partners.

## 10. Financing risk

A company may require further capital and may be unable to raise it on acceptable terms. Failure to secure financing may lead to insolvency, distressed financing, severe dilution, asset sales, or shutdown.

## 11. Regulatory and legal risk

A company may operate in regulated markets or across countries with different licensing, securities, privacy, employment, consumer, tax, sanctions, export, intellectual property, competition, or other requirements. Non-compliance may materially affect operations and value.

## 12. Tax risk

Investment, holding, conversion, transfer, disposal, dividend, token, referral, or other activity may have tax consequences. GBlock does not provide tax advice.

## 13. Currency and cross-border risk

Investments and exits may involve foreign currencies, capital controls, banking restrictions, sanctions, withholding tax, cross-border settlement, or legal systems that differ from the Investor User's home jurisdiction.

## 14. Fraud and misconduct risk

Private markets may involve fraud, fabricated metrics, undisclosed conflicts, misuse of funds, false customer claims, cap table disputes, side agreements, or other misconduct. Platform controls cannot eliminate these risks.

## 15. Secondary transaction risk

A secondary seller may not have good title or may lack required transfer approvals. Secondary transactions may involve information asymmetry, stale valuations, transfer restrictions, and settlement risk. GBlock does not settle secondary trades under this Agreement.

## 16. No recommendation

A profile, introduction, score, Data Room, KYC result, visibility ranking, badge, or communication on GBlock is not a recommendation to invest and is not a statement that the opportunity is suitable for any User.

## 17. Independent diligence

Investor Users should independently review corporate existence, authority, cap table, ownership, financials, liabilities, litigation, IP, contracts, customers, technology, cybersecurity, tax, regulation, offering exemption, transaction documents, transfer restrictions, and other matters material to their decision.

## 18. Ability to bear loss

An Investor User should participate only if the Investor User can bear the economic risk, including total loss and long-term illiquidity, and after obtaining appropriate professional advice.
