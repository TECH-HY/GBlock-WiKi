# SCHEDULE 10 - ACCEPTANCE, VERSIONING AND EVIDENCE FRAMEWORK

## 1. Purpose

This Schedule explains how GBlock may obtain and evidence electronic acceptance. It supports the contractual architecture described at the beginning of this Agreement.

## 2. Registration acceptance

At registration, GBlock may require a User to affirmatively accept Part I and the mandatory policies incorporated into account use. The registration interface should provide a conspicuous link to the current Agreement before acceptance.

## 3. Privacy acknowledgement

GBlock may require an acknowledgement that the User has read the Privacy Policy. Such acknowledgement does not replace a separate consent where consent is the legal basis for a particular processing activity.

## 4. Marketing consent

Marketing opt-in should be separate and optional. Refusal of optional marketing must not prevent creation of an otherwise eligible GBlock account.

## 5. Cookie consent

Optional cookie or analytics consent should be managed through the cookie settings interface rather than bundled into general contractual acceptance where applicable law requires a separate choice.

## 6. Scoring purchase acceptance

Before payment for a scoring service, GBlock may present:

(a) service description and price;
(b) link to Schedule 2 and Schedule 4;
(c) refund and cancellation information; and
(d) where legally required, a separate request for immediate commencement and acknowledgement of any effect on cancellation rights.

## 7. Data Room acceptance

Before first access to a restricted Data Room, GBlock may present the Data Room Owner's identity and require affirmative acceptance of Schedule 3 and any additional NDA.

A separate acceptance may be required again if the Data Room confidentiality terms materially change.

## 8. Referral acceptance

Before a User activates the Referral Program, GBlock may require affirmative acceptance of Schedule 5.

## 8A. Introduction and deal-protection acceptance

The general commercial-protection obligations in Section 12 and Schedule 11 may form part of mandatory Business User account terms. Where a specific introduction carries a fee, success-based payment, exclusivity term, special Tail Period, or other transaction-specific commercial obligation, GBlock should, where practicable, present the relevant Introduction Terms before the introduction or before disclosure of identifying contact information and record the User's affirmative acceptance.

GBlock may also issue an Introduction Notice after an introduction to memorialise the date, parties, opportunity, and applicable Tail Period. An Introduction Notice does not create a new fee unless the User has separately accepted the fee basis.

## 9. Re-acceptance

GBlock may require re-acceptance after a material terms update, material privacy change requiring acknowledgement, new service-specific terms, or another event where renewed acceptance is appropriate.

## 10. Acceptance records

Subject to applicable privacy law, GBlock may retain:

- User and account identifier;
- organisation identifier where applicable;
- document and policy version;
- effective date;
- text or identifier of the acceptance statement;
- timestamp;
- IP address;
- user agent or device information;
- country or location information reasonably derived for compliance;
- session or transaction identifier;
- checkbox or button action;
- Data Room identifier where applicable;
- Protected Introduction or Introduction Notice identifier where applicable;
- Protected Party or opportunity identifier where applicable;
- accepted Introduction Terms and applicable Tail Period where applicable;
- order or payment identifier where applicable;
- marketing consent status and withdrawal time;
- cookie choice records;
- document hash or integrity identifier; and
- later re-acceptance, withdrawal, or policy-version events.

## 11. Electronic signature and conduct

Where applicable law recognises electronic contracting, a User agrees that an electronic acceptance action may evidence intention to be bound to the applicable terms. GBlock may require additional authentication where a higher assurance level is appropriate.

## 12. Copies and accessibility

GBlock should make the accepted Agreement reasonably accessible to Users electronically and may allow download or provide archived versions on request where appropriate.
