# SCHEDULE 2 - SCORING TERMS AND DISCLAIMER

## 1. Nature of the GBlock score

1.1 A GBlock score is an informational product generated by applying a defined methodology to available inputs.

1.2 Within the same rubric version, configuration, and input snapshot, the scoring methodology is designed to produce consistent outputs from the same inputs. Different rubric versions, data updates, correction rules, or system changes may produce different results.

1.3 A report may state the generation date, rubric version, data-current date, and other metadata. Those items form part of the report and should remain attached when the report is shared.

## 2. What the score is not

A GBlock score is not:

(a) investment advice;
(b) a recommendation to buy, sell, subscribe for, or avoid a security;
(c) legal, tax, accounting, audit, assurance, or financial advice;
(d) a regulated credit rating or rating-agency opinion;
(e) a fairness opinion or valuation opinion;
(f) due diligence on behalf of an investor;
(g) a guarantee of fundraising, traction, solvency, success, liquidity, exit, or investment return; or
(h) an endorsement by TECH HY.

## 3. Input limitations

3.1 Scores depend on available inputs. Inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, unsupported, manipulated, or selectively submitted inputs may produce misleading outputs.

3.2 GBlock does not independently verify every input. Verification of one fact does not imply verification of all facts.

3.3 Company Users are responsible for the truthfulness and lawful submission of their inputs.

## 4. Evidence and substantiation

4.1 GBlock may request evidence supporting a claim used in scoring.

4.2 If evidence is not supplied, is inconsistent, or materially undermines a claim, GBlock may disregard the input, reduce evidential weight, recalculate the score, label the item unverified, suspend publication, or withdraw the report.

4.3 GBlock may preserve substantiation records where reasonably necessary to support the integrity of the scoring service or resolve a dispute.

## 5. Freshness and stale status

5.1 A score is based on information available as of its generation or data-current date.

5.2 If underlying information exceeds the Platform's freshness threshold, which may be 30 days for a particular product version, GBlock may display a stale label or other warning.

5.3 A stale label does not mean the score was accurate before it became stale, and absence of a stale label does not guarantee that all information remains current.

## 6. No duty to continuously update

Unless a separate paid service expressly provides continuous monitoring, GBlock has no duty to continuously update a Scoring Report after generation.

## 7. Methodology changes

GBlock may modify scoring criteria, weights, data requirements, anti-gaming controls, or presentation prospectively. GBlock is not required to disclose security-sensitive anti-manipulation logic or proprietary implementation details.

## 8. Corrections

A Company User may request correction of a factual input by providing reasonable evidence. GBlock may investigate and determine whether recalculation is appropriate. A correction request does not guarantee a higher score.

9.1 User-supplied underlying company data remains subject to the ownership rules in Part I.

9.2 TECH HY owns the report format, scoring methodology implementation, report text generated by GBlock, design, labels, and related GBlock Content, subject to third-party rights.

9.3 GBlock grants the paying User a non-exclusive licence to use and share an unaltered Scoring Report for legitimate business, fundraising, diligence, or internal purposes, subject to this Agreement.

9.4 A User must not remove disclaimers, alter a score, remove a stale label, edit the report and present it as official, or imply that GBlock guarantees the Company User.

## 10. Withdrawal and correction by GBlock

GBlock may withdraw, supersede, correct, or mark a report where GBlock reasonably believes the report is materially misleading, based on false inputs, affected by manipulation, legally restricted, or no longer appropriate to display.
