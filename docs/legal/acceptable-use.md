# SCHEDULE 6 - ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

## 1. General rule

You must use GBlock lawfully, honestly, securely, and consistently with the purpose of the Platform.

## 2. Illegal and regulated activity

You must not use GBlock to:

(a) commit or facilitate unlawful activity;
(b) conduct unlawful securities dealing, brokerage, investment management, financial promotion, solicitation, market manipulation, insider dealing, or other regulated activity;
(c) launder money, finance terrorism, evade sanctions, or disguise beneficial ownership or source of funds;
(d) breach export controls or court orders; or
(e) facilitate another person's evasion of a legal restriction.

## 3. Fraud, deception and misrepresentation

Prohibited conduct includes:

(a) fake demand signals or fabricated investor interest;
(b) false revenue, customer, partnership, pipeline, team, funding, valuation, ownership, licence, audit, or traction claims;
(c) falsified documents or altered evidence;
(d) impersonation;
(e) deceptive account identities;
(f) manipulating scoring inputs; and
(g) intentionally withholding information in order to make a submitted claim materially misleading.

## 4. Privacy and confidentiality abuse

You must not:

(a) upload personal data without authority or lawful basis;
(b) disclose Confidential Information in breach of a duty;
(c) scrape personal data;
(d) publish doxxing material;
(e) use Data Room materials beyond the Permitted Purpose; or
(f) upload Data Room content to unauthorised AI or third-party tools.

## 5. Cybersecurity and technical abuse

You must not:

(a) upload malware or malicious code;
(b) attempt unauthorised access;
(c) probe, scan, penetrate, or load-test systems without written permission;
(d) bypass rate limits, authentication, paywalls, access permissions, or security controls;
(e) interfere with Platform availability;
(f) exploit a vulnerability for purposes beyond responsible reporting;
(g) exfiltrate tokens, credentials, session data, or confidential files; or
(h) automate high-volume requests in a manner that degrades service or violates published API terms.

## 6. Scraping and data harvesting

Automated scraping, crawling, harvesting, bulk exporting, mirroring, or creation of a competing database from Platform data is prohibited unless GBlock expressly authorises it.

## 7. Spam and abusive communications

You must not send unsolicited bulk messages, harassment, threats, unlawful marketing, deceptive referral promotions, phishing, or repetitive unwanted communications.

## 8. Intellectual property

You must not upload, reproduce, distribute, or use content in violation of intellectual property rights or remove GBlock proprietary notices.

## 9. Multiple accounts and identity manipulation

You must not create or control deceptive multiple accounts, use another person's account, purchase accounts, sell accounts, or create accounts to manipulate referrals, access, scoring, or moderation.

## 10. Circumvention of controls and commercial misuse

You must not evade geographic restrictions, investor eligibility controls, sanctions screening, Data Room permissions, account limits, referral rules, or enforcement measures.

You must also not:

(a) use GBlock primarily as a source of curated identities, contact details, counterparties, or opportunities and then intentionally conceal or divert a resulting Protected Transaction to avoid obligations owed to TECH HY;
(b) hide a Protected Transaction, misstate the identity of a transaction party, use an undisclosed Affiliate or nominee, or change transaction structure for the purpose or material effect of avoiding valid Introduction Terms;
(c) harvest or systematically extract GBlock's curated relationship network for unrelated off-platform solicitation; or
(d) encourage, assist, or conspire with another person to commit commercial circumvention prohibited by Section 12 or Schedule 11.

Direct off-platform communication with a Protected Party is not prohibited merely because it occurs outside GBlock. The prohibited conduct is the bad-faith avoidance of obligations owed to TECH HY or misuse of GBlock's curated introduction service.

## 11. Prohibited content

You must not upload content that is unlawful, malicious, intentionally deceptive, infringing, or designed to facilitate fraud, credential theft, malware distribution, or other prohibited activity.

## 12. Enforcement framework

Depending on seriousness, history, and risk, GBlock may use:

1. warning or request for correction;
2. content restriction or removal;
3. feature restriction;
4. temporary suspension;
5. reward freeze or forfeiture where the violation concerns referrals;
6. Data Room revocation;
7. permanent account termination; or
8. referral to authorities where required or appropriate under law.

Severe fraud, sanctions risk, material confidentiality breach, cyberattack, identity fraud, or unlawful financial activity may justify immediate action without a prior warning.

## 13. Notice and appeal

Where permitted by law and appropriate to the circumstances, GBlock will provide the reason for a material enforcement decision and an internal appeal channel. An appeal may be reviewed by a person not principally responsible for the initial decision where reasonably practicable.

GBlock is not required to disclose information that would compromise security, fraud controls, confidential third-party information, legal privilege, law-enforcement activity, or regulatory restrictions.
