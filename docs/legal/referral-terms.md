## 1. Participation and definitions

1.1 Participation is optional and may be restricted by country, account standing, verification requirements or programme capacity. Registration is open; an invitation or separate application is not a general condition of access. Participation creates no employment, agency, brokerage, partnership, fiduciary or franchise relationship.

1.2 The definitions in the [Master Platform Terms](https://gblock.club/legal/terms) apply. Payment balance is non-withdrawable service prepayment. Reward balance is the separate balance of referral rewards subject to this Schedule.

## 2. Attribution and qualifying revenue

2.1 A personal referral link or code attributes genuine new Users according to the programme rules. The referred User must decide independently to participate and make a qualifying service payment that clears. Self-referrals, fabricated accounts, invalid payments and abuse do not qualify.

2.2 Qualifying confirmed service revenue includes scoring bought by card or Payment balance, paid Data Room Subscription cycles and purchased Vouchers. A top-up to Payment balance does not itself create a reward; a later qualifying purchase using that balance may do so. Registration, invitations, activation or recruiting alone earn no reward.

2.3 Fees or commissions calculated from the value of a completed deal, including revenue from a sale block or its lifecycle, are not qualifying revenue and earn no reward, as also stated in Section 23.2 of the [Master Platform Terms](https://gblock.club/legal/terms).

2.4 Do not market rewards as compensation for selling, recommending, arranging or soliciting securities. No reward scheme creates authority to perform regulated activity or accept investment funds.

## 3. Direct rates and weekly snapshots

3.1 Current direct reward rates by rate-eligible reputation tier are:

- Verified: reputation 0-49, 10%.
- Core: reputation 50-149, 13%.
- Principal: reputation 150-349, 17%.
- Partner: reputation 350-749, 20%.
- Vanguard: reputation 750 or more, 25%.

3.2 The direct rate is capped at 25% and fixed by the weekly rate snapshot. A mid-week reputation change does not change an already fixed weekly rate. The percentage is applied to qualifying GBlock service revenue, not credited simply for bringing in a User.

3.3 The total reward allocation for a qualifying revenue event is capped at 25% of that revenue. Where qualifying sponsor-line rewards consume part of that budget, the direct component is reduced to the available remainder. A quoted direct tier rate is therefore not a guarantee that the full nominal percentage will be credited in every sponsor configuration.

3.4 GBlock may change published rates and prospective programme rules. A later rate change alone does not retroactively reduce legitimately earned rewards; invalid underlying payments, corrections and lawful abuse findings remain subject to Sections 7 and 9.

## 4. Sponsor lines

4.1 Eligible first-, second- and third-line sponsors receive 6%, 3% and 1%, respectively, of the qualifying confirmed GBlock service revenue on which the direct rate is calculated. Each sponsor must have confirmed KYC. These shares operate within the 25% total reward budget in Section 3.3, rather than being an unrestricted addition to the direct rate.

## 5. Holding period, balances and transfers

5.1 A qualifying reward becomes eligible for payout after the 30-day holding period measured from the underlying revenue, subject to verification, account standing, reservations and this Schedule. Eligibility is not an instant payout.

5.2 Eligible Reward balance may be requested for payout or transferred to Payment balance with a 3% service-credit uplift. That uplift applies to a transfer, not to an external payout. The same applicable KYC limit applies to reward transfers. Payment balance and Top-up bonuses cannot be withdrawn and earn no interest.

5.3 Customer Service cannot promise to zero Reward balance or perform a payout on your behalf. Use the supported payout or transfer procedure. Any resulting Payment balance remains service prepayment, including on a later account-deletion request.

## 6. Payout process and verification limits

6.1 Payouts are operator-processed in USDT on the BEP-20 network. No other network or payout currency is promised. USDT is a payout rail only; GBlock does not provide custody, exchange, investment, staking or trading services through this programme.

6.2 The minimum request is USD 50 equivalent. You may make one payout request per calendar month. The operator responds within one Business Day and pays no later than 20 Business Days after the request, subject to lawful holds and the verification and standing rules below. A Business Day excludes Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

6.3 An open payout request reserves its amount. Reward balance is debited when the operator sends the payout and supplies the transaction hash, rather than merely when the request is submitted.

6.4 Without confirmed KYC, the applicable aggregate limit is USD 150 over a rolling 30-day period, assessed across the payout address and linked account group. Verification is also required for sponsor-line rewards and may be required below any threshold where reasonably necessary. The same limit applies to eligible reward-to-Payment balance transfers. The threshold is not a statement that verification is legally unnecessary below it.

6.5 GBlock may request identity, wallet, source, tax or other compliance information, and may hold or refuse a payout where required by law, sanctions requirements, fraud risk, provider requirements, unresolved identity issues or a reasonable investigation. This is not a representation that scoring performs sanctions screening or that every compliance check is automated.

6.6 You must provide a compatible wallet address on the supported BEP-20 network. Blockchain transfers may be irreversible; wrong addresses or unsupported networks may cause unrecoverable loss. Network fees, congestion, outages, forks or provider limits may affect transfers. Digital-asset fiat values can vary. You remain responsible for applicable legal and tax obligations.

## 7. Reversals, disputes and debt

7.1 Sections 22.4 and 22.6 of the [Master Platform Terms](https://gblock.club/legal/terms) govern payment reversals, dispute phases, review and debt. A related reward is cancelled proportionately for an invalid or reversed underlying payment during the holding period; subsequent clawback, including of paid rewards, may create debt. A valid reversal correction is distinct from punishment for exercising a payment right.

7.2 While a dispute about your own payment is open, new rewards do not accrue, payouts and reward transfers are frozen, recurring charges are suspended and deletion is deferred. Following a lost dispute those restrictions continue pending operator review; invitations, sureties and guarantees are additionally paused during that review. User-initiated card payments remain available. Review timing is governed by Section 22.6 of the Master Platform Terms; this Schedule does not set a different period.

7.3 A lost dispute alone does not establish abuse or impose an automatic ban. Review restrictions are lifted when abuse is not confirmed, subject to independent lawful restrictions; debt remains until settled or written off. A punitive reward sanction requires an operator-confirmed abuse finding and the applicable enforcement process.

7.4 Adjustments for already spent Vouchers and balance-funded Subscription cycles require manual correction before the first reward payout. Automatic clawback for every such case is not promised. GBlock must verify the applicable adjustments before releasing affected rewards.

## 8. Anti-abuse rules

The following are prohibited:

- Self-referrals and duplicate, fake, synthetic, bot, stolen, purchased or controlled accounts.
- Misleading promises about investments, returns, GBlock or rewards.
- Spam, unlawful direct marketing, scraping and purchased contact lists.
- Paying a referred person solely to create a fake qualifying account.
- Cookie stuffing, attribution manipulation, link hijacking, impersonation and technical manipulation.
- Unlawful securities solicitation and collusion to generate rewards without genuine service use.

## 9. Investigation, sanctions and appeal

9.1 GBlock may temporarily hold disputed rewards while investigating suspected abuse, fraud, identity issues, payment validity, sanctions requirements or technical errors. An interim hold is not a final abuse determination.

9.2 Rewards attributable to invalid payments, refunded fees or fabricated referrals may be corrected under Section 7. Confirmed fraud, self-referral or material programme abuse may lead to cancellation of the related reward entitlement and proportionate programme or account sanctions under the Master Platform Terms. This does not promise an operator tool to erase Reward balance.

9.3 Where reasonably possible and legally permitted, GBlock provides reasons and an appeal route for a material reward-sanction decision. Legitimate exercise of a statutory payment right alone is not programme abuse.

## 10. Taxes and no authority

10.1 Rewards may be taxable. You are responsible for reporting and paying taxes unless law requires GBlock or a provider to withhold or report. Payouts are sent by GBlock, operated by TECH HY SDN. BHD.

10.2 You must not claim to be GBlock's employee, broker, agent or authorised representative, bind GBlock, make warranties on its behalf, change its prices or terms, promise investment returns or regulatory status, or use its marks except as expressly permitted.

## 11. Programme changes and termination

11.1 GBlock may suspend, modify or discontinue the programme prospectively. Legitimately earned and cleared rewards remain payable subject to this Schedule, applicable law, verification, fraud controls and payout availability. There is no guarantee of earnings or investment outcomes.

11.2 This Schedule must be read with Section 23 of the Master Platform Terms. Participation and payout acceptance requirements must be implemented consistently; this candidate does not represent that every reward recipient has already accepted a versioned Schedule or that every payout currently checks such acceptance.
