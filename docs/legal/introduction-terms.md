# SCHEDULE 11 - COMMERCIAL INTRODUCTIONS AND ANTI-CIRCUMVENTION TERMS

## 1. Purpose and application

1.1 This Schedule protects the commercial value created when TECH HY identifies, curates, introduces, connects, re-connects, or facilitates access between Users, buyers, sellers, issuers, shareholders, investors, acquirers, partners, service providers, or other counterparties.

1.2 This Schedule applies to Business Users that use GBlock introduction functionality or receive a Protected Introduction. A Consumer is bound only to the extent applicable law permits and the relevant terms are validly incorporated or separately accepted.

1.3 This Schedule does not prohibit Users from communicating or transacting directly with each other. Its purpose is to prevent bad-faith avoidance of obligations owed to TECH HY and misuse of GBlock's curated relationships, not to restrain lawful trade or business.

1.4 Business Users acknowledge that TECH HY may invest substantial time, cost, know-how, relationship capital, screening effort, curation, coordination, and operational resources in identifying and facilitating counterparties. The protections in this Schedule are intended to preserve the agreed commercial value of those services and the evidential record of the introduction.

## 2. Protected Introduction

2.1 A Protected Introduction has the meaning in Section 12. It may occur even if TECH HY does not participate in later negotiations or closing.

2.2 A Protected Introduction includes situations where GBlock supplies enough information for a User to identify or contact a counterparty, including through a profile, opportunity summary, curated list, Data Room, message, event, meeting, email, call, group, or direct introduction.

2.3 A Protected Introduction may also include a material re-introduction or reactivation of a dormant relationship where TECH HY materially facilitates renewed substantive engagement concerning a new or substantially different opportunity.

2.4 TECH HY does not claim ownership of a person's identity or general business relationships. The contractual protection concerns TECH HY's introduction, curation, facilitation, confidential or restricted access, and the obligations the User accepted in connection with that service.

## 3. Protected Parties

3.1 The initially introduced person or entity is a Protected Party.

3.2 Protection also extends, to the extent involved in the same or a substantially related opportunity or transaction, to that party's Affiliates, controlled funds, acquisition vehicles, special-purpose vehicles, beneficial owners, controlling persons, nominees, and other entities through which the relevant transaction is pursued or completed.

3.3 Protection does not automatically extend to an unrelated business merely because the same adviser or professional representative acts for both businesses.

## 4. Protected Transactions

4.1 A Protected Transaction includes any transaction or commercial relationship described in Section 12.7 that arises from or is materially facilitated by a Protected Introduction.

4.2 A Protected Transaction remains protected if its structure changes after the introduction. Without limitation, a share purchase may become an asset acquisition, a direct purchase may be completed through an acquisition vehicle, an investment may be completed through an Affiliate, or a proposed transaction may be divided into several related agreements.

4.3 Where applicable Introduction Terms identify a narrower transaction scope, those terms govern the fee scope. This Schedule does not expand a specifically agreed fee beyond its stated commercial basis.

## 5. Non-circumvention obligation

5.1 A User must not, directly or indirectly, circumvent, bypass, avoid, or attempt to avoid TECH HY for the purpose or with the material effect of avoiding an obligation that applies to a Protected Introduction under this Agreement or applicable Introduction Terms.

5.2 Prohibited conduct may include:

(a) obtaining a Protected Party's identity or contact details through GBlock and intentionally moving the relationship off-platform in order to conceal a fee-triggering event;
(b) asking or encouraging a Protected Party to contract through an Affiliate, nominee, related person, or alternative vehicle to avoid applicable Introduction Terms;
(c) deliberately failing to disclose a fee-triggering closing or completion after accepting a duty to notify TECH HY;
(d) mischaracterising, fragmenting, postponing, substituting, or restructuring a transaction primarily to avoid a valid fee or other commercial obligation owed to TECH HY;
(e) using a new entity, fund, special-purpose vehicle, employee, adviser, shareholder, beneficial owner, or other intermediary to pursue substantially the same Protected Transaction while asserting that the introduction no longer applies; or
(f) colluding with another person to hide the existence, value, parties, status, or completion of a Protected Transaction.

5.3 A User does not breach this Schedule merely by communicating directly with a Protected Party, negotiating without TECH HY, using its own advisers, declining a transaction, or choosing a lawful alternative transaction structure, provided the User does not act to avoid obligations owed to TECH HY.

## 6. No obligation for TECH HY to participate in negotiation or closing

6.1 TECH HY need not remain copied on communications, attend negotiations, draft transaction documents, participate in diligence, or be involved in closing for a Protected Introduction or Protected Transaction to remain subject to this Schedule.

6.2 Direct dealings between Users do not constitute a waiver by TECH HY of valid Introduction Terms unless TECH HY expressly waives them in writing.

## 7. Tail Period

7.1 Unless different Introduction Terms apply, the default Tail Period is 24 months as described in Section 12.10.

7.2 The Tail Period is intended to define the duration of TECH HY's commercial-protection rights arising from the introduction and is not a prohibition on the User carrying on a lawful business.

7.3 A transaction signed during the Tail Period remains subject to applicable Introduction Terms even if closing or payment occurs after the Tail Period, where the fee trigger or applicable Introduction Terms so provide.

7.4 Account closure, termination, suspension, migration to another communication channel, or deletion of a conversation does not shorten a Tail Period that has already begun.

## 8. Prior relationship procedure

8.1 A User that had a substantive pre-existing relationship with a Protected Party concerning the same or a substantially related opportunity should notify TECH HY within 5 Business Days after the Protected Introduction or Introduction Notice.

8.2 Reasonable evidence may include dated substantive correspondence, meeting records, transaction discussions, mandates, signed agreements, CRM records created before the Protected Introduction, or other contemporaneous evidence showing genuine prior engagement.

8.3 The following do not by themselves establish a substantive pre-existing relationship: knowing of the person's existence, a social-media connection, being on the same mailing list, attending the same public event, unresponded cold outreach, or historical contact unrelated to the relevant opportunity.

8.4 If timely evidence demonstrates that the relevant transaction arose wholly independently from a substantive pre-existing relationship and did not materially use TECH HY's introduction or facilitation, TECH HY will not assert commercial protection solely because a later GBlock contact also occurred, unless separately agreed Introduction Terms state otherwise.

## 9. Introduction Notice and objections

9.1 TECH HY may issue an Introduction Notice recording the date of introduction, Protected Parties, opportunity description, Tail Period, and a reference to applicable Introduction Terms.

9.2 A Business User should notify TECH HY within 5 Business Days of any material factual error in an Introduction Notice, including an asserted substantive pre-existing relationship.

9.3 Failure to object does not create a fee that was never agreed. However, subject to applicable law, TECH HY may rely on an unchallenged Introduction Notice and its underlying records as prima facie evidence of the recorded introduction facts.

## 10. Progress and completion reporting

10.1 Where lawful, a Business User must provide the notice required by Section 12.12.

10.2 If applicable Introduction Terms include a fee calculated by reference to an agreed transaction metric, the User must provide information reasonably sufficient to verify the calculation when the relevant trigger occurs.

10.3 TECH HY may request a written certification from an authorised representative confirming whether a Protected Transaction has signed, completed, closed, or triggered an expressly agreed payment obligation.

10.4 TECH HY may request reasonable supporting evidence limited to what is necessary to verify compliance. A User is not required to disclose legally privileged material, information whose disclosure is unlawful, or unrelated confidential information that is not reasonably necessary for verification.

## 11. Fee protection

11.1 No fee is created solely because a Protected Introduction or Protected Transaction exists. A fee is payable only if the User has accepted Introduction Terms or another agreement that states the applicable fee or payment basis.

11.2 Where valid Introduction Terms provide for a fee, the User may not avoid that fee by moving discussions off-platform, excluding TECH HY from communications, using an Affiliate or intermediary, changing transaction form, or otherwise engaging in conduct prohibited by this Schedule.

11.3 If the contractual fee trigger occurs in relation to a Protected Transaction during the applicable Tail Period, the fee becomes payable in accordance with the accepted Introduction Terms as if the transaction had been transparently disclosed and completed without circumvention.

11.4 If more than one agreement or transaction forms part of a single integrated commercial arrangement, applicable Introduction Terms may apply to the integrated arrangement only to the extent their wording reasonably covers it. TECH HY is not entitled to double recovery of the same fee basis.

11.5 No transaction-based fee, commission, placement fee, brokerage fee, or success fee relating to securities or another regulated financial product is payable under this Schedule unless it is expressly set out in a separate lawful agreement and TECH HY has determined that the relevant compensation arrangement and services may lawfully be offered and performed. If such compensation would be unlawful, this Schedule does not require it to be paid.

## 12. Records and evidential framework

12.1 The parties acknowledge that introduction disputes may arise after communications move outside GBlock. TECH HY may therefore retain lawful electronic records described in Sections 1.5 and 28 and Schedule 10.

12.2 Subject to applicable rules of evidence, TECH HY may rely on Platform logs, Data Room access records, emails, messages, CRM records, meeting invitations, calendar records, introduction records, accepted Introduction Terms, and Introduction Notices to establish the chronology and scope of a Protected Introduction.

12.3 Unless a User timely established a substantive pre-existing relationship under Section 8, a transaction with a Protected Party during the Tail Period may be treated as prima facie connected with the Protected Introduction where TECH HY's records show material introduction or facilitation. The User remains entitled to rebut that inference with contemporaneous evidence showing that the transaction arose wholly independently and did not materially use TECH HY's introduction or facilitation.

12.4 A User must not destroy, falsify, conceal, or manipulate records for the purpose of defeating this Schedule or avoiding a payment obligation.

## 13. Remedies and enforcement

13.1 Deliberate circumvention, concealment of a fee-triggering Protected Transaction, or material misuse of a Protected Introduction is a material breach of this Agreement.

13.2 Subject to applicable law, TECH HY may exercise contractual remedies including suspension or termination, preservation of relevant records, recovery of an amount already contractually due under valid Introduction Terms, and a claim for compensatory loss or damage caused by the breach.

13.3 TECH HY may seek reasonable external legal, investigation, collection, arbitration, or enforcement costs to the extent those costs are recoverable under this Agreement, applicable law, an arbitral award, or court order.

13.4 Where legally available and appropriate, TECH HY may seek interim, injunctive, preservatory, disclosure, or other equitable relief to prevent continuing misuse, destruction of evidence, unlawful disclosure, or dissipation of assets relevant to an asserted contractual claim.

13.5 Remedies are cumulative to the extent permitted by law, but TECH HY is not entitled to double recovery for the same loss.

## 14. Regulatory safeguard

14.1 This Schedule governs contractual commercial protection and does not expand the regulatory services TECH HY performs under Section 6.

14.2 TECH HY is not required to negotiate a transaction, transmit an order, handle client money, settle a securities transaction, recommend an investment, or perform another regulated activity in order to preserve contractual rights arising from a Protected Introduction.

14.3 If application of a provision to a particular transaction would require TECH HY to perform an activity that TECH HY is not legally permitted to perform, that provision will be interpreted as narrowly as reasonably necessary to avoid requiring the unlawful activity, without invalidating other lawful commercial-protection provisions.

## 15. Survival

15.1 This Schedule survives account closure, termination, suspension, and cessation of Platform use for the applicable Tail Period and for any longer period reasonably necessary to enforce a payment obligation or claim that arose during that Tail Period, subject to applicable limitation law.

15.2 Confidentiality, records, dispute resolution, indemnity, and payment provisions that otherwise survive under this Agreement continue according to their own terms.
