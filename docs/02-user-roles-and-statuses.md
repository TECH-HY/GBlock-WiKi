# 2. User roles and statuses

GBlock has one account per person. What that account can do is described by
**three independent axes**: your **status**, your **trust level** and your
**reputation tier**. Almost every "why can't I do X" question is answered by
checking which axis is blocking you.

| Axis | Question it answers | Values |
|---|---|---|
| **Status** | How did you enter, and what base rights do you have? | Ambassador, Residency applicant, Resident |
| **Trust level** | How proven is your identity? | Anonymous, Vouched, Verified |
| **Reputation tier** | How much reputation have you built? | Member, Core, Principal, Partner, Vanguard |

The axes are independent. A Resident can be Anonymous (no KYC). A Vanguard
member can be Vouched rather than Verified. Your access at any moment is the
combination of all three.

---

## Status

Everyone who registers starts as an **Ambassador** with a welcome grant of
reputation. After your first sign-in you choose your **paths** in the cabinet.
Paths can be combined and added later; each one asks for its own consent.

| Path | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Data Room & Scoring** | Open Data Rooms for your company and use project scoring. |
| **Ambassador** | Invite people you can stand behind and earn referral rewards. |
| **Apply for Residency** | Join the waitlist and collect the vouches you need to become a Resident. |

| Status | How you get it | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| **Ambassador** | Register with your email | Cabinet, invitations and referral rewards, Data Rooms, project scoring, read-only view of the Secondary Market and the Demand Board, the wallet. No dealflow. |
| **Residency applicant** | Take the Apply for Residency path | A place on the waitlist and the right to collect vouches. |
| **Resident** | Two vouches from existing members, the decisive one from a Resident, then approval of your waitlist request | The dealflow, placing blocks, mediated introductions, deal threads, posting on the Demand Board, and the right to vouch for others. |

Ambassador is about **community growth**; Resident is about **market
participation**. A member can be both.

### Welcome grant

- **30** reputation points when you register directly.
- **40** points plus **one vouch gift** when you register through a member's
  invitation. The inviter's reserved points come back to them when the
  invitation activates.

### Becoming a Resident

Resident status is earned through the waitlist. You collect **two vouches** from
members who have passed KYC; the decisive second vouch must come from an
existing Resident. A vouch gift received with an invitation counts as a real
vouch. Your own KYC does not change your status, but it adds trust in deals
(see below), and a Resident with verified KYC can also open Data Rooms about
other companies.

The only exception is a one-time launch stage: the operator appoints the first
Residents directly, in a limited number, so that the network has members who can
vouch. These grants cannot be requested, and a granted Resident has the same
status and the same right to vouch as everyone else.

## Trust level

Trust level lets members take part at the level of identity assurance they are
comfortable with, from anonymous to fully verified.

| Level | How you reach it | What it unlocks in the market |
|---|---|---|
| **Anonymous** | Default | Browse the dealflow and express buyer interest. Cannot place sale blocks. |
| **Vouched** | Two guarantees from Verified members | Place sale blocks, on a 24-hour freshness window. |
| **Verified** | Pass KYC | Place sale blocks without a guarantor, on a 72-hour freshness window; vouch for others; request larger payouts; and build reputation faster (+20%, _rolling out_). |

Good to know:

- Trust level matters even for Residents. A Resident who is Anonymous can browse
  and express interest but cannot sell until they are Vouched or Verified.
- To place a sale block you must also be the founder of record of the company
  and hold a valid scoring on your own Pro Data Room. The operator does not
  moderate blocks before they go live; it steps in afterwards if there is a
  complaint or a dispute.
- A sale block stays current for a limited window (72 hours for Verified sellers,
  24 hours for Vouched sellers) and must be renewed before it goes stale.
- KYC is run by an external provider. GBlock never sees or stores your
  documents. You are never forced to reveal your identity to other members.
- One identity can be verified only once. See
  [Trust and safety](07-trust-and-safety.md#kyc-and-identity).

## Reputation tier

Your tier comes from your **reputation** (called Volume in the cabinet). It sets
your reward rate and, for Residents, how many free Data Rooms you get. It cannot
be bought.

| Tier | Reputation | Direct reward rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Member** | 0 - 49 | 10% |
| **Core** | 50 - 149 | 13% |
| **Principal** | 150 - 349 | 17% |
| **Partner** | 350 - 749 | 20% |
| **Vanguard** | 750+ | 25% |

The ladder has no ceiling; 25% is the maximum direct rate. Your rate is
refreshed once a week. The word _Verified_ always means the KYC trust level,
never a reputation tier. More in [Reputation economy](05-reputation-economy.md).

## Data Room functions: the founder side

"Founder" is not a role you apply for and not a badge. It is a silent extension
of your account that unlocks the selling side of the platform: a payment balance
for top-ups and scoring purchases.

- **Anyone can open a Data Room** for their own company, and the first one is
  free.
- **It switches on by itself** when you, as room owner, upload the first
  document to a Data Room of your own company. There is no application and no
  waiting period.
- **It is lifelong and invisible.** No badge appears anywhere; the functions
  simply become available.
- **It does not change your reputation** and does not by itself allow
  withdrawals.

Details: [Data Rooms, scoring and subscriptions](12-data-rooms-scoring-and-subscriptions.md).

---

## One person, one account

GBlock keeps a single account per real person. Buyer and seller are modes inside
that account, not separate registrations, and opening a new account does not
clear a sanction _(rolling out)_. This is what makes shared accountability real:
the reputation cost attaches to the person, not to a throwaway handle.
